व्य = वि + अ + ञ
व्य = bird net 
[[वि]] = bird (if we take व as wave/flapping movement, mouth if we take व as speech
[[अ]] = here 
[[ञ]] = net  ([[लोप]]) 

 व्य = [[व]] + [[य]] 
व = wind, wave, mix, speech, 
य = join, connect, copulate
Wave-like movement join = cover
wind connect - blow away - spend/reduce

[[वि]] + [[अय]]
wave inward + here connect = wash away
wind (objectified) + here connect = connected to wind = blown away
wind inward + connect here = inlet for wind
wind (objectified) + here connect in meta sense = wind like

wave (objectified) + here connect = bird here connect = net
wave (objectified as in wave like movement) + here connect = cut
speech (objectified) here connect/join
