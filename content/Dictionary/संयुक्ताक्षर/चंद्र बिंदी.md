चंद्र बिंदी can take any of following meanings - individually or jointly
[[म]] = inside = Torsion ?
[[ञ]] = surface = Sheer? 
[[ङ]] = outside, periphery = bending? 
[[न]] = pull = Tensile load
[[ण]] = push = compressive load
