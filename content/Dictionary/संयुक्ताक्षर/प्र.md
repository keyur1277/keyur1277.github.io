Commentary
[[प्र]] in वर्ण/वर्ण-समास  [[Era]] प्र means vine/rope growing in water.  This meaning continues thru वर्ण समास epoch uptoडुकृञ् epoch where it evvolves into its उपसर्ग use. 
Hence, as the building block of प्रत्यय, "प्र" means rope/vine growing in water.
As उपसर्ग it takes रूढ अर्थ.
