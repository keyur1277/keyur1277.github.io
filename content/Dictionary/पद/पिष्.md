पिषॢँ = [[प]] [[इ]] [[ष]] [[ऌ]] 
press/push with limb using body mass (प)  below (इ) toe/top (ष) - subjugate
leg pointed subjugated
foot toe subgugated = giving pressure of the limb + below + toe + subjugate 
rope object  tip subjugated
