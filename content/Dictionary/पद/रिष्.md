ऋषं = [[ऋ]] [[ष]] [[चंद्र बिंदी]]

superior needle point objectified (इ as स्वर विभक्ति) 

commentary
As superior (ऋ) + abstract(objectify-इ(needle point-ष)), ऋषि means someone who is superior and goes first into unknown and to whom others follow like a thread. This, of course, is later day interpretation when abstraction abilities evolved. 
Pre-abstraction era meaning would have been more like (superior pointed outside), akin to a leader with better weapons leading the group.
