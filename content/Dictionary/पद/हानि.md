comment
Though क्तिन् is not [[निष्ठा]] it behaves like one here and त becomes न . Also it is [[कित्]] but does not cause हा into ही.
"Float/log" becomes "pull". But "upto" does not become "inward"
"separation point - (inside/outside/surface/push/pull) - hard up to stone" + क्तिन् here means "stone float below pull"
Because क does not mean maneuver in क्तिन्, हा does not become ही (upto hard does not become hard inward)
ति does become नि. It is float becoming noose and not in log becoming noose/pull in mooring post sense. 

Because reality behaves differently in context of rocking he raft; this is निपातन / anomaly. 
 हानी is not captured as निपातन in अष्टाध्यायी but recorded so along with others in ग्लाम्लाज्याहाभ्यो निः
