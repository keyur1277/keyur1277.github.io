[[7.3.36]] 

If instruction is "push below" (णि) and it comes  after  "up to" (आ) insert "footing maneuver" (पुक्)  in between. "up to  push below" (आ + णि)  is to be read as "after footing maneuver push below" आ + पुक् + णि
