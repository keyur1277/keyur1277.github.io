 Commentary
 याच् refers to asking/offering, something bundled/ to be bundled in a hide. 
 The following are the most ancient technology manuals. They teach bundling something inside a hide by knot and gaining it back by un-knotting the bundle. (gaining it back because ibject permeance may or may not have existed)

 ज ञ = open surface/hide = ज्ञ
य आ (च)  ञ - याच 
य ज ञ = यज्ञ = The word was already mythicized and lost its वर्ण-based meaning by the time texts we have access to were considered worthy of preservation.  
The literal meaning of यज्ञ is the connect to wide/opening of skin. Besides evident spread skin as seat meaning,  Skin could mean waterskin and opening could mean mouth of the bottle. 


 These interpretations as activities manual and not as words - predate संज्ञा revolution. This is the evolution phase between the Great Apes and early Homo. (Great ape- early Homo  timing based on Doikika, Gona, Olduvai Gorge)
