प्रीति = [[प्री]]  + [[क्तिन्]]
originally it meant rope+water accomplished.
Reed net + maneuver log + inward pull = reed net + gathered
It also used to mean life accomplished - sex - meaning joy, pleasure, happiness, satisfaction.
Later this meaning survived and the earlier one faded out.
