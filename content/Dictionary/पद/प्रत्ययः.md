प्रत्ययः

[[प्र]] is a prefix indicating natural as opposed to सन indicating desired and वि indicating alternate.
[[प्र]] = natural
[[त्य]] is like "-ed" of English. It is कृत प्रत्यय directly applied to प्र that normally is उप सर्ग . 
त्य - past participle 
त्य = that joined and hence "-ed"
[[य]] is its वर्ण meaning - join/connect. 

प्रत्य =  that which has naturally arisen / something that has come forth by itself
प्रत्यय = natural-ed connection

Also, [[प्रति]] + [[अय]]
reflection + connect here 

Also
rope+water float connected joined = join (rope + water,  float + tumbi)

Commentary 
[[प्र]] is a rope/vine growing in water. Another name for that is नल - Common Reed.
