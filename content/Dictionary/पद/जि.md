जि = [[ज]] [[इ]] 
open/widen/spread objectified
open below/inward
spread below/inward
widen below/inward
jump below/inward

रूढ अर्थ 
जय = जि + अच् = circular reference.
जय = [[ज]] [[य]] = open join = plug the opening.
comment: traditionally it has been clarified that जय here does not mean winning. But as [[उत्कर्ष]]  
अभिभव = [[अभि]] + [[भू]] + [[अप्]] = here steady below + steady ground up + here footing = opening
न्यूनीभवन and न्यूनीकरण  = [[नि]] + [[ऊन्]] + [[अच्]]  
भाषा = [[भाष्]] + [[अङ्]] + [[टाप्]] = steady at needle + here outside + coracle till foot/rope = refers to opening in boat
