घञ्
hunt surface - hunting ground
hunting net or hunt skin (in घस्य  ञ sense) 
hyde 
hence indicates संज्ञा-करण objectification 
[[घ]] = hunt
[[ञ]] = net/surface/skin



Example: 
अलङ्कार = [[अलम्]] + [[कृ]] + [[घञ्]]
अलङ्करण = [[अलम्]] + [[कृ]] + [[ल्युट्]]  


Comments
As प्रत्यय, घञ् means संज्ञा करण indicating product/result/instrument/output 
On other hand ल्युट् means संज्ञा करण indicating process. 
I consider घञ् as waterskin and ल्युट् as seep hole. ल्युट् is the process to obtain water and घञ् is the carried - संज्ञा-fied - water. 
Use of these as प्रत्यय predates [[तिङ्]] but occurs after संज्ञा revolution for sure, and perhaps after डुकृञ् revolution.
