आमन्त्रण = [[आङ्]] + [[मन्त्र]] + [[णिच्]] + [[ल्युट्]] 
noun (up to periphery + cause(inside pull place) )
noun for causing inside pull (मन) place upto periphery = extending मन place to periphery. 


रूढ अर्थ 
1. Calling or calling to - extending mind place
2. Courtesy, welcome, adieu = extend mind place
3. Invitation
4. Interrogation.
5. from meaning of गुण = speak intelligently
