क्तिन् = [[क]] [[त]] [[इ]] [[न]] 
maneuver that inward pull 
Because maneuvering something inward by pull is equivalent to gather, this is a स्त्री प्रत्यय 

क्तिन् = [[क्त]] + [[इन्]]
[[क्त]] = क् त
accomplished, hand held tumbi, hand held that, stone that, hand-held floating
maneuver+float 

इन् turns  पद [[स्त्री]] so, क्तिन् is  क्त in स्त्री, ie. accomplished in gathered /spread sense. 

As pratyaya it is accomplished with undertone of inward pull
