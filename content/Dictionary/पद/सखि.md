comments
There are so many rules governing सखि as प्रातिपदिक distinctly, its antiquity seems to go back to pre-धातु era.
सखि is पुल्लिंग but साखी is स्त्रीलिंग. सखि  is risk taking and सखी  is relatively low risk/ gathering activity. Though this classification could have arisen only after distinction in [[ह्रस्व]] and [[दीर्घ]] became distinct. 
I am tempted to interpreted as सह + ख्या in context of [[आख्या]]. But that would be possible only if word emerged after tradition of शिक्षा - on a second though this is not necessary शिक्षा could have adopted existing word. There is another similar example in word - संज्ञा. सखि as a word must have been in extance and important for it to leave so many निपात in शिक्षा tradition.
