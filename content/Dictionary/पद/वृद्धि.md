### वृद्धि

What is वृद्धि ?
 
With वृद्धि (i.e. extension) - 
अ becomes आ = here becomes upto
इ becomes ऐ  = inward becomes connected 
उ becomes औ = outward becomes at/on 
ऋ becomes आर् = superior becomes until/upto blood/water
ऌ becomes आल्= submission becomes until/upto soft/wet/limp/taken


Refer [[वृद्ध]] 
[[वृद्धि as पद]]   = [[वृध्]] + [[कि]] or [[क्तिन्]] or [[क्तिच्]]


Comment
One possibility  is - 
Originally, Only अ इ उ and ऋ ऌ were available anatomically.
अ encoded what later evolved into अ and आ.  आ evolved last amongst स्वर.  Originally अ was not part of गुण वृद्धि frame work. On availability of आ, it was interjected. 
इ encoded what later evolved into इ ए and  ऐ 
उ encoded what later evolved into उ ओ and औ 
ऋ encoded what later evolved into ऋ अर and आर 
ऌ encoded what later evolved into ऌ अल and आल. 

The first evolutionary step was गुण- literally meaning "verb(move) push", where ए ओ अर अल split  (evolved from अ इ उ  ऋ ऌ) by push-triggered-move - articulatorily (the tongue moves) and conceptually (the object moves.)
further evolution/development/expansion (वृद्धि) is second split where ऐ, औ,आर,आल are outcome of push beyond गुण - once again both articulatorily (the incremental tongue moves) and conceptually (the object expansion). 

I am considering आ to be retro fitted because as per my current understanding आ was not available untill larynx descent and it occurred much later (~430 Ka if we go by Sima de los Huesos) in evolution.
