उदात्त
उ = outward OR up
दा = roll/give till
त् = that 
त = that/class marker 
property/class of (roll up that) = higher (in upper sense but not in superior ऋ sense, that would be ऋत)
up/outward roll upto that tumbi 
like (outward give till that)

रूढ अर्थ 
1. Gentle and plenty, Generous
2. giving, giver, great, illustrious, loved, noble
3. Accented - "raise" accent

Commentary
Imagine two तुम्बी held by two persons. Each has a long vine attached to each.
Now consider instructions - outward give till that tumbi" - This could be process of intertwining vines. could be. 

As a meta instruction it could be "like (outward/above give till that)" - this indicates object that is to be moved outward/above up to another object.  [[6.1.197]] indicates that this is also a valid meaning. Although this certainly is later day addition because imho उदात्त अनुदात्त and स्वरित distinctions were non existent with Homo Erectus anatomy.  For Erectus, action had to accompany speech to indicates which object is above  which one is below and which one is at level. 

When "like (outward/above give till that)" is considered in alms/helping others context, this means noble also.
