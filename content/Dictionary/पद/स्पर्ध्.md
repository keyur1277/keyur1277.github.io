comment:
स्पर्धँ and सङ्घर्ष referred to teeth and tongue in one of their inbterpretation. This meaning is lost except in meaning of ह्वे. 
they also meant struggle if we consider different meanings of प and ष in both worfds respectivey. And that meaning is extant.
Also earlier meaning got lost as we no longer eat or drink the way we used to when स्पर्धँ and सङ्घर्ष referred to teeth and tongue. As we started using hands most of the time, the other use faded away.
