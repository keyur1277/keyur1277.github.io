गन्धँ = [[ग]] [[न]] [[ध]] [[चंद्र बिंदी]]
live pull carry inside 

धातु अर्थ 
अर्दने = [[अर्द्]] + [[ल्युट्]]

रूढ अर्थ 
1. injure, hurt, kill - in the fishing sense
2. ask, beg
3. move
4. smell - later-day meaning coming from smell. Originally, smell meant the smell of fish and was considered smell of food.
