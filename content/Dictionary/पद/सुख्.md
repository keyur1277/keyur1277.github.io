सुख  = [[स]] [[उ]] [[ख]] 
verb(furrow) hollow
gathered outward hollow 
verb(gather) hollow = referring to drying food. 

Comments
Originally - in ice age, storing food in a hollowed out ice could have meant सुख in cold temperatures Or it could have meant drying gathered food in non cold climates also.
It could have meant making furrow also.
Later day सुखट in Marathi, सुखड़ी in Gujarati, सूखा, etc suggest association with dried food.
