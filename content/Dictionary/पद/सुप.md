title  :  "सुप"
---
सुप  
gathered/straight outward rope/limb  
to gather/straighten rope/limb  
to furrow rope/limb  

  
[[स]] gathered, aligned, straight, furrow 
[[उ]] outward or स्वर विभक्ति 
[[प]] rope, limb, sprout

Commentary 
सुप originally also called पद are primordial "nouns"। These are basically abstractions arising out of and related to fishing.
