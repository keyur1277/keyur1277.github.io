नदी = [[नद्]] (णदँ)+ [[ङीप्]]  
push give + object(outside) limb  

Refer to [[नदी संज्ञा]]  for use as a indicatory label used by पाणिनि 

comment
घि नदी सखि are three संज्ञा- names given to gathered materials स्त्री संज्ञा. 
घि = object(hunted) - not moving objects or objects nearby
[[नदी]]  = push give + object(outside) limb = ==beyond reach of limb==
[[सखि]]  = gathering exist + hollow connect till + pull = ==containers==
