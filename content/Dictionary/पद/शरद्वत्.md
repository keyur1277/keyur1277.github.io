शरद्वत् = शरद् + वत् = [[शॄ]] + [[अदि]]  + [[मतुप्]]  
Erect/Javeline superior surface +  Here give object / here roll in / here give below + inside float above foot (in containing/having sense)
superior javeline surface + here give inward + inside float above footed

Comments
शरद् means giving superior javeline below inward. to achieve श+ऋ you need to श+अर. Here water  makes javeline superior. with superior javeline, you add instruction here give inward/below. This is plough.
शरद् ended up refering to a season when that act of ploughing occured. post monsoon and before land becomes total dry.
Use of the same wood for music as वीणा (person objectified push desire) is plausible.
This interpretation is, of course, only 10 ka old. Originally शरद् meant something else for lakhs of years when language, and most likely thhis word, existed and agriculture did not.
