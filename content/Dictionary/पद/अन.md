अन as suffix under [[सुत्रपाठ/7/7.1/7.1.1]]
[[अ]] =  here 
[[न]] =  pull 
remove hence  negation
आदेश  for  [[नञ्]]
[[न]] =  pull
[[ञ्]] =  surface/net
remove net /surface, hence negation
as stand-alone and as अप-भ्रंश of  नञ् it has the same meaning - negation in the removal sense
