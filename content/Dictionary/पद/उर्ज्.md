up superior jump 
up superior jump push 
up/above straight


धातु पाठ 
बलप्राणनयोः =
बल = contain (ब) + sotf (ल) here, sot means material that is to be chipped
प्राणन =  प्र + अण +ल्यूट = objectified (limb flow push)
So बलप्राणनयोः refers to contain (note that it is not holding with क or प) material to be chipped and strike (push with extended limb flow form up/above)
