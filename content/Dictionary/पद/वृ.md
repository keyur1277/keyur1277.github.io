वृ = [[व]] [[ऋ]]
superior person
superior speech
superior wave
wind superior
make superior
superior  flapping/waving movement


धातु अर्थ 
at संवरण = [[सम्]] + वृ + [[ल्युट्]] = equal wave movement objectified = cover
at वरण = वृ + [[ल्युट्]] = suopoerior person objectified = selection
at [[सम्भक्त]] = devoted / shared
at [[आवरण]] = आङ् + वृ + ल्युट् = objectify(upto/untill wave like movement prevails)

रूढ अर्थ 
1. to select, choose = वरण
2. appoint, invite = superior person


commentary
Except for सम्भक्त, all other meanings are self-referenced.
