object(throw) object(rope) inside/surface/outside/pull/push 
throw inward rope inward inside/surface/outside/pull/push
roll inward rope inward pull - refers to wick making

धातु अर्थ 
self/circulatory reference

रूढ अर्थ 
shine glow = refers to wick making and meaning is reverse reference from wick accomplished. 

Commentary
Wick making was such a  powerful technology, it obliterated all previous meanings of दीप. 
If दीप् is not relevant as lamp, the word using दीप् is almost certainly pre-wick making era.
On other hand, if we are understanding words in pre-lamp making era, दीप् is to be interpreted as rolling/throwing/giving rope and not as lamp/wick.
