This is part of पृषोदरादि -ancient enpugh to be accepted as निपात 
षष् perhaps meant thorn and finger. meaning removing nail.
and सो + क्विप् fits this description. However by the time it is used as षष्ठी - continuous connection sense, meaning has reversed and षष्ठी refers to nailing something together - exactr opposite of what षष् could have meant and what सो + क्विप्  almost certainly means. 

Fact that सु /सो potentially refers to same act, indicates very very deep origin - when स्वर distinction itself was not yet established. Along with स्वर there is fuzziness in स ष also. 
Naturally, neither स ष उ ओ are original sounds when these words emerged. Likely different species alltogather. But potentially biped or anatomy requiring assisted birth.
