---
title : "क्ष"
---
क्ष = [[क]] [[श]] or [[क]] [[ष]]
hand held stake
hand held javeline
hence aim
hand held pointed



Comment
Besides "hand held javeline", क्ष  also means "maneuver erect."
When communication was amply aided by hand maneuvers, in नृत era, क्ष could have meant aim in meaning sense also.
