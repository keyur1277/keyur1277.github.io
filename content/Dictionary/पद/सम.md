सम 
[[स]] Equal, gather, furrow   
[[म]] Inside, fish   
Hence  
Compound, together  
Equal inside  
Internally Equal  
Inside furrow  
gather(ed) inside  

Because ञ न म ण ङ change their form depending on subsequent वर्ण, सञ preceeding चु (च छ ज झ), can be सन सम सण or सङ. Same for सङ preceeding कु (क ख ग घ), सम preceeding पु (प फ ब भ),  सन preceeding तु (त थ द ध), सण for टु (ट ठ ड ढ). 
In this context, consider the following - 

सङ = [[स]] [[ङ]] = gather outside, parallel outside, straight/align outside, furrow outside 
सञ = [[स]] [[ञ]] =  (gather, furrow, parallel, straight) x (surface, hyde, cloth, net)
सन =[[स]] [[न]] = (gather, furrow, parallel, straight) x (noose, pull,, below)
सम = [[स]] [[म्]] = as described above
सण = [[स]] [[ण]]  = (gather, furrow, parallel, straight) x (push, shovel)
