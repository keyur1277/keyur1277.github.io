Originally फ meant floating and प meant hanging-footed-based. Whole concept of base/footing being a function of hanging in arboreal world. 
These meanings underwent two stage changes. The first is when प started tomean footing on ground in stead of footing with rope. The second change came when फ meaning was reassigned to log - त. meaning "flot" got reassigned to  त which till then meant log. फ atrophied.
