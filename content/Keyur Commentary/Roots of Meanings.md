## Abstract
Saussure is one of great linguists of recent past. He proposed that there is no natural inherent or logical connection between a word's form and its meaning. signifier and signified are arbitrarily assigned social convention. Saussure did not believe that everything is assigned. But assigned signifier are core and once this core is in place, derivation may also occur. 
This is foundation stone of most of modern linguistic theory. 
Pāṇini is a great linguists of not so recent past. Pāṇini  described a logical system by which all Saṁskṛta words are derived using smaller components and words derive their meanings from its constituents. The components, however, have meanings assigned thru social hereditary convention. Pāṇini narrows the universe of  signifier for which meaning is assigned thru arbitrarily social convention.

This paper presents a hypothesis that Varṇa/Akshar have intrinsic meanings and components used by Pāṇini as building blocks have meanings derived rather then assigned. Though author believes that Varṇa meanings themselves are not socially assigned. they are rooted in evolution of brai., Being beyond social of this paper, that question is left open for future research.

This hypothesis proposes that words (signifier) have evolution like genealogy and not Genesis like genealogy as proposed by Saussure. 

## Hypothesis and its testing
In Saṁskṛta all words (Pad) are derived words. Building blocks from which final words are derived  are -- upasarga, dhātu, pratyaya. Pāṇini describes [^1]  different processes applied depending on concepts.  Pāṇini also gives exhaustive list of exceptions to process.
Saṁskṛta has a very strong tradition that word meanings are derived from word constituents. But this process of derivation stops at list of upasarga, dhātu, pratyaya. 
The hypothesis this paper present is - Saṁskṛta varṇa have intrinsic meanings and upasarga, dhātu, pratyaya are not assigned their meaning/usage but have derived meanings arising from varṇa meanings.
These varṇa meanings are lost and need to be deciphered. If all words are derived words, meanings of  वर्ण can be deciphered form words made of the  वर्ण.

If the hypothesis is true; upasarga, dhātu and specially pratyaya meanings should generate a coherent meaningful interpretation as derived from its वर्ण constituents' meanings. Because there are ~2k धातु, 22 उपसर्ग and ~2 to 4k प्रत्यय, random match for large portion of these is statistically impossible simultaneously while keeping polysemi of वर्ण compact/coherent.

## Decipher Process
#### Curiosity Phase
Somewhere between 2022-2023, while listening to the word वसुदेवसुतंदेव, curiosity arose that Varṇa can have their own meanings and words may derive their meanings from these constituent meanings. 
Out of that curiosity,  Varṇa meanings were deciphered. This was done before encountering work of Pāṇini. Deciphering was done using  Gujarati, Hindi and Sanskrit.
All 13 Svar were excluded form decipher process as they can combine with any Consonant. A grid of of 33 consonants was made to arrive at total of 1089  varṇa combinations, each consisting of two consonants. Real words having this pair and additional Svar was sought in dictionary. From this list of known words having this consonant pair,  varṇa meaning was guessed. At this phase Svara and their function was not known. Part of decipher was also inspired by similarities between Bramhi and Proto-Sinaitic scripts.
#### Decipher Phase
Study of Varṇa led to works of Pāṇini. Already deciphered were tested on list and meanings of धातु. Fitment was tried keeping agriculture or at the best hunting gathering as context. Meanings of  व्यंजन were refined to align with known meanings of धातु. 
somewhere between July-August 2024, ऋ and ऌ were deciphered treating them as अस्पृश्य & स्वर. This lead to decipher of entire स्वर portion and realization that स्वर were predecessors to the function assumed by विभक्ति in descriptions of Pāṇini.
With स्वर as potential विभक्ति markers, धातु were checked for fitment with extant decipher of वर्ण. Realization occurred that there are multiple layers of context and the ancient most layer decisively predated age of agriculture by a huge margin. वर्ण meanings were refined to pre agriculture context.
म न ण  were deciphered early on but around this period ङ was deciphered based on अङ्ग, अङ्गुल and English. ञ was understood from its established meaning as शुक्र.
By late 2025, वर्ण meanings were committed to git as a time stamped reference.

## Frame work for interpretation
Following frame work is reference point for interpretation
### Step-1: पाणिनि Layer
1. All संस्कृत पद can be derived in two ways - (1) as पद derived thru पाणिनि system of derivation - we will refer to this kind of derivation as Pad derivation. (2) as  वर्ण sequence/soup - We will refer to this kind of derivation as वर्ण समास.  This is discussed in step 2
2. Pad Derivation leads to उपसर्ग धातु प्रत्यय and पाणिनि processes that result in final पद. Meaning of पद is derived form its constituents. Process is deemed meaning less. This leaves us to derive meanings of उपसर्ग धातु प्रत्यय. 
3. Meanings of  उपसर्ग धातु and प्रत्यय can be derived only as वर्ण string/soup - वर्ण समास process. 
### Ste-2: वर्ण समास Layer
In this step वर्ण समास meanings are derived from from वर्ण meanings. Following are guidelines in this regards.
1. All धातु in मूल रूप, रेगुलर प्रत्यय, उपसर्ग, आगम, आदेश, लकार, other प्रत्यय including उण्आदि and  निपात are to be interpreted as वर्ण समास. 
2. Distinctions of तिङ-सुप्, काल, वचन, पुरुष, लिङ्ग are to be ignored and वर्ण meaning is fungible across all these distinctions.
3. वर्ण looses noun and verb distinction and represents both simultaneously
4. स्वर could be interpreted as वर्ण or as  स्वर विभक्ति. स्वर can work as विभक्ति to its predecessor and subsequent वर्ण
5. आ and अ are interchangeable. अ added to हल  (व्यंजन) is ignored
6. दीर्घ ह्रस्व प्लुत are interchangeable
7. उदात्त अनुदात्त स्वरित are interchangeable
8. Entire of context of वर्ण meaning is referenced by वर्ण. 
9. Meaning of वर्ण समास is to be understood with help of context. The context where ALL constituent वर्ण meanings would be held in immediate use working memory of some Homo or Great Ape sometime between 7 Ma and 1 Ma is the context referred to by that वर्ण समास. 
10. अनुस्वार can be interpreted as any nasal

## Conclusion
Assumption that language emerged on 7th day of arbitrary assignment is unreasonable. Language resembles complex living forms and must have evolved. 
The non random nature of Sanskrit and consequent total derivability from वर्ण gives us two fields to work on - (1)  वर्ण meanings to पद  must give evolutionary history of language and consequently mankind. My early findings suggest language predates Homo sapiens and most primitive evolutionary form of language predates homo genus. (2) वर्ण emerge from brain circuitry and cognition. They have deeper roots that can be identified.
