==Hypothesis based on reinterpretation of textx based on decoded meanings==

- Spoken language started long long before writting emerged. Spokeen language consists of phonemes - वर्ण. 
- वर्ण has a cluster of meanings that it refers to.
- This meaning is rooted in brain circuitry and hence not acquired but innate
- वर्ण are of two types - स्वर & व्यंजन.
- This spoken language started evolving 70 lakh years ago with स्वर. व्यंजन became available earliest by ~40 lakh years ago. Not all स्वर & व्यंजन evolved in one go. They evolved over time and became available at different times. 
- वर्ण समास is a combination/sequence of वर्ण 
- वर्ण समास derives its meanings from constituent वर्ण 
- Meaning of वर्ण समास is derived meaning and not assigned meaning
- Communication using वर्ण समास is possible without teaching its meanings upfront.
- This is how language boot strapped. One can communicate even when other person was not trained on language and had no prior knowledge of meaning of given sound sequence. Meaning could be arrived at using meanings associated with component वर्ण. and वर्ण themselves had meanings built into brain circuitry of the specie/genus. 
- Use of these वर्ण समास allowed for transmission and compounding of knowledge. 
- Later  वर्ण समास are also combined to convey second level derived meanings.
- some वर्ण समास were used so frequently to modify/add/change meanings of other  वर्ण समास, they started to loose their original context and were used metaphorically. These evolved to become उप सर्ग and  प्रत्यय. Some change in their constituent वर्ण depending on original meanings and usage evolved into what we now refer as आगम and आदेश. Something akin to "underst==and==" and "overst==ate==." The stand, original remembered as स्थ in Sanskrit and ष्ठा in its वर्ण समास form, is changed depending whether one is standing under or above.
- This is the period when formal education of language started. Tradition evolved to teach  pronunciation, वर्ण meanings and established वर्ण समास  in context of their physical meanings. this institutionalizing education, specially that of language and processes, compounded knowledge further and specie started to become more and more wealthy and wise. This teaching material, along with all subsequent additions,  is what we now inherit as शिक्षा and व्याकरण. By extension  छंद, कल्प, ज्योतिष and निरुक्त should also belong to same tradition. 
- Over period of time the teaching corpus kept on accumulating. the tradition gave extra ordinary focus to preserve old teaching material unadulterated and add new materials on either side of the corpus - begining or end depending on whether new addition was definition kind of addition or modification kind of addition. Definitions were added before old corpus and modifications were added later. 
- Some वर्ण समास are 30-40 lakh years old. But start date for maintaining the teaching material seems to be some time before ~15 lakh years. 
- Most of corpus sems to have been in place by some time between 5-15 lakh years ago.
- There are subsequent additions to the text but original corpus is largely available as it was. Though context and meanings are lost and need to be deciphered.
- Decipher of these texts has lead me to believe this -
	- Language evolved on sea shore fishing settlements. This occurred before obligatory bipedalism and is most likely cause of obligatory bipedalism, loss of fur and high sodium preference that Homo lineage has.
	- Fishing and boating are the main context on which  deciphered meanings of तिङ् & सुप् प्रत्यय वर्ण समास can be understood
	- There was not distinction between verb and noun earlier. This differentiating emerged with boating. तिङ् are about launching and docking boats & सुप् are about making boats. 
	- concept of tense and moods in grammar evolved from need to predict  tides and food availability and edibility. These लकार evolved in two distinct phases.
	- This evolution occurred at a place that had sea on west, marshy lands covered with reed type plants on east, river flowing from north and meeting sea in south.
