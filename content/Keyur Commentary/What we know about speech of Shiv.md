Known facts 
* There was high nasalisation
* Tonal manipulations were absent
* Breath control was rudimentary or absent. (ह्रस्व दीर्घ प्लुत) are absent
* Maximum two/three phonemes plus one nasal can be combined in one breath. 
* Consonants could be pronounced without a trailing vowel. The trailing vowel was not mandatory. अ at end was not default.
* back-low vowels were difficult/indistinguishable
* with higher epiglottis and larynx, it was possible to  peak whille drinking eating
