छिदिँर् = [[छ]] [[इ]] [[द]] [[इ]] [[चंद्र बिंदी]] [[र]]
छि = (not walking, not repeating,  not knotted, drill, moving at one place only; many+tiny) + below/objectified
दी = (roll/throw/give) + below
चंद्र बिंदी for ञ or ङ or म = surface/outside/inside
र = sharp
Hence
drill below roll/give below  sharp = cut below, hew off, rend, split, pierce, separate 


धातु अर्थ 
at द्वैधीकरण =  द्वैध making = द्व + ऐ as स्वर विभक्ति (षष्टि = contineuous connection) + ध =द्व स्य ध = carrying of [[द्व]] 
It can mean carrying of two.
It can also mean carry of give wave like movcement.
