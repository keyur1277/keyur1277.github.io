
तिङ् प्रत्यय are sub section of [[लस्य]]. 
तिङ् प्रत्यय are instruction set referring to action required to dock and launch boats. the action required depends on three parameters.
1. Whether purpose is to launch or dock boat this is direction of oar (पद). 
2. anchorage - on beach, in basin or in deep sea. The word for anchor is - verb(rope) water up pointed = पुरुष 
3. Draft of the ship. This is measured in single wave equivalent, two wave equivalent or more than two waves.

==[[पद]]==
पद mans paddle/oar. There are two directions in which one can paddle. towards shore and towards sea. Hence there are two types of पद - [[आत्मनै]] and [[परस्मै]]  

==[[पुरुष]]==
There are three locations where a boat can be anchored. [[प्रथम]]  पुरुष means anchoring on/near beach. [[मध्यम]] पुरुष means anchorage which is like basins adjacent to pier. [[उत्तम]] पुरुष means deep sea anchor.

==[[वचन]]==
वचन refers to draft of the boat measured in height of wave/person. [[एक]] वचन means draft is such that you can receive boat with hands/legs [[द्वि]] वचन means draft is such that it needs rolling in wave - tide.  [[बहु]] वचन means draft is such that boat is constrained hard/tight outward.
