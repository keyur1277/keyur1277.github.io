### गुण

what is गुण?

with गुण - 
अ becomes आ = here remains here
इ becomes ए  = inward becomes to/into/संप्रदान/ receive/connect
उ becomes ओ = outward becomes from/अपादान give/detach
ऋ becomes अर = superior becomes here blood/water/flow
ऌ becomes अल = submission becomes here wet/limp/sand 

Meaning expained
When you move-push (as against move-pull):
in inward direction it results in receiving-संप्रदान 
in outward direction it results in detachment-अपादान 
in superior direction it results in ?This blood = bloody 
in submission direction it results in "this take=taken" or "this limp"
