# कच्/पच् धातु-श्रेणी — Notes on Antiquity Evidence

## The four roots
- **पच्** (डुपचँष्) — cooking
- **पञ्च्** (पचिँ, चुरादिः, विस्तारवचने) — spread/extend; source of "five"
- **कच्** (bare) — maneuver + knot
- **कञ्च्** (कचिँ, भ्वादिः, दीप्तिबन्धनयोः) — encircling, binding

All four share प/क (limb-movement) + च (knot/circle/again) as a common core, with variant उपदेश-forms (डु-, -ँष्, -इँ) marking different historical stages of the same root rather than unrelated words.

## Key findings

**नुम् (nasal insertion) as chronological marker**
पचिँ/कचिँ (nasalized) vs डुपचँष्/कच् (non-nasalized) are variants of one root, not separate roots. नुम् = ङ् (outside/periphery), and its presence/absence tracks a real semantic shift — e.g. पक्ति (bare act) vs पङ्क्ति (act's outward/collective extension → row, group).

**ष (pointed/finger) — dating evidence**
ष appears only in डुपचँष्'s इत्-marker, absent from पचिँ/कच्/कचिँ. Explanation: प was originally usable as a forelimb capable of fine digit-manipulation (counting-adjacent action) — hence डु ("beware") was required, since the support-limb was momentarily committed to a non-locomotor task. Later, प anatomically specialized to hind-limb/locomotor-only function, making finger-manipulation with प impossible — so both ष (the manipulative/pointed component) and डु (the beware-marker tied to that risky act) dropped together in the later variant. This is treated as a structural prediction (two features lost jointly, for one causal reason), not a coincidence.

Supporting reference: [कप य] page — क and प were originally interchangeable limb-movement types (both achieving य), later specializing: क → hand/manipulative (reaching, grasping), प → leg/locomotor (propulsion, posture). सूत्र 3.1.13's क्यष् (leg-finger manipulation still labeled क-type) supports movement-type, not anatomy, as the deciding factor.

**Revised "five" hypothesis**
The earlier "five = counting on fingers via प" reading is invalidated in its original form (ष isn't present in पञ्च्'s own उपदेश, पचिँ) but is restored once डुपचँष्/पचिँ are treated as the same root's earlier/later variants — the counting sense belongs to the *earlier* (forelimb-capable, ष-bearing) stage.

**कच् anatomical constraint**
कच् (bare, no इ/चंद्र-बिंदी) and कचिँ (encircling) converge on the same meaning because great-ape finger curl is anatomically uni-directional (inward-closing only) — this rules out an "outward maneuver" alternate reading for bare कच्, so both variants land on "encircling" without इ/ँ being redundant (they make explicit/object-bound what anatomy already forces).

**घोर = घ(hunt) + ओ(from) + र(water)** — "hunting from water" = fishing in the broad sense. र's water-sense (over its alternate fire-sense) is selected on chronological grounds: fishing is argued to predate fire (archaeological premise), so fire-based र is ruled out for this word specifically.

**Dating chain (partially supported)**
Fishing must be at least as old as the oldest confirmed hominin seafaring evidence (Sulawesi/Wallacea tools, 1.04–1.48 Ma, per 2025 Nature study) — since seafaring presupposes fishing competence. This gives fishing an antiquity floor of roughly up to ~15 lakh years (upper end of that range, not a settled point). This floor is proposed as the earliest boundary for the नुम्/घोर-अघोर split, tied to the transition from hunting to fishing.

## Open/unsolved
- **"Fame" for पङ्क्ति**: unsolved, and likely a non-issue — Monier-Williams itself flags पङ्क्ति's "fame/cooking" senses as an incorrect conflation, properly belonging to **पक्ति**, not पङ्क्ति. Fame from पक्ति's own components remains undecoded.
- **English "count" ← कञ्च्**: explicitly held as unverified hypothesis, not a claim — standard etymology derives "count" from Latin *computare*, with no shown sound-correspondence to कञ्च् so far.
- **सम्+पत्र → Latin computare** (nasal-loss reading): componentially coherent (सम्=gather inside, पत्र=limb+place) but the *specific* nasal-loss date (5–15 lakh years) merges two separately-sourced figures (seafaring floor ~15 lakh; larynx descent ~5 lakh) whose link to this specific nasal event is not yet established — flagged as open.
- **व = person** (used in तिङ् decipherment, ध्वम् = carry-person-inside): confirmed present in व's own dictionary entry (speak → speaker → person chain).
