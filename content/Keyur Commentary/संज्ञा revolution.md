### संज्ञा revolution
(This is a hypothesis.  It will take more work to prove it to others. I dont have time or energy, at present, to prove it partly because, for me, this is sufficiently proven, and partly because I have so much work on, this has moved to low priority once i have conviction.)

Identifying a sound sequence to belong to a uniquely identifiable class (वर्ण) is essential to communicate thru sound. 
The cognitive unit in which sound is classified is वर्ण. 
When _Hominina_ split from _Homininae_, for some reason, the temporal (time) length of वर्ण that can be uniquely identified, reduced, compared to other _Homoninidae_ like _Panini_. 
As a result, multiple वर्ण could be held in temporary working memory.
This enabled the use of multiple वर्ण-समास to be fit in working memory. 
वर्ण-समास is not a वर्ण but a pointer to set of वर्ण. 
This is equivalent to the invention of a pointer as a variable.
This is recursion. 
Selection bias in evolution resulted in this recursivity to strengthen over generations and eventually become totally abstract and limitless. 
I believe this happened through [^1]BGCT loops. Genetics gives infrastrucgture but does not activate the potential. That depends on exposure to language. 

[[संज्ञा]] revolution is this ability to "refer" to a bundled meaning. Ability to treat the bundle as an object without unpacking its contents.  
When _Homo Erecrus became aware ofthis ability to name/point things, he called it bundling - संज्ञा. 
The usage (of word संज्ञा)  as an abstract concept eventually erased this original physical meaning of संज्ञा as a "bundle."
