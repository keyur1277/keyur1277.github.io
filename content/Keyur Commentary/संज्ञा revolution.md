### संज्ञा revolution
Identifying a sound sequence to belong to a uniquely identifiable class (वर्ण) is essential to communicate thru sound. 
The cognitive unit in which sound is classified is वर्ण. 
When Hominina  split from Homininae, for some reason, the temporal (time) length of वर्ण that can be uniquely identified, reduced, compared to other Homoninidae like Pan. 
As a result, multiple वर्ण could be held in temporary working memory.
This enabled use of multiple वर्ण-समास to be fit in working memory. 
वर्ण-समास is not a वर्ण but a pointer to set of वर्ण. 
This is equivalent to  a invention of pointer as a variable.
This is recursion. 
Selection bias in evolution resulted in this recuirsion ability to strengthen over generation and eventually become totally abstract and limitless.
