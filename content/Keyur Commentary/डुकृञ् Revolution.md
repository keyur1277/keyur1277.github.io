
डु[[कृ]]ञ् is primordial sentence, pun intended. It means - Be ware ([[उ]] of [[ड]]) maneuver/stone ([[क]]) superior([[ऋ]])  surface ([[ञ]]).  It is also sentence in punishment sense because it has triggered an infinite loop in OS of human mind.

By ~33 lakh years ago (goin by evidence at Lomekwi),  Homonini had figured out how to make and use stone tools.
Up to this point, like rest of animals, Hominin default brain LOOP was something akin to REPL (Read-Eval-Print Loop) in Unix. In animals, we can say instead of print the default output was muscle movement, what we now refer to by word "act" - for animals it is like REAL (Read-Eval-Act-Loop) .   So the OS was at rest unless triggered by environment. 

There were always some individuals whose resting loop was "Seek-Act-Evaluate-Loop" (SAEL) These were monkeys always seeking to "do" something, irrespective of whether required or not. Kids whose explore function never turned off. In normal world, i assume, such individuals ended up in mouth of lions more often and their population remained in check. Most of specie remained in idle loop if environment did not change.

My hypothesis is that this stone tool event caused individuals with SAEL loop focused on action to be preferentially selected by evolution. Tool user had advantage over non user. As a result downstream Hominini and consequently nearly all Humans have this loop as default setting. One continuously seeks to do something, irrespective of whether it is required or not. 
The resting infinite loop in brain OS for most humans is "urge to do", something डुकृञ् reflects in language; instead of "urge to observe" for most animals. 

Because this loop is motivation loop and is rooted in limbic - animal brain - it is beyond direct control of "thinking" cortical brain.

I believe this is when language started to differentiating verbs from hitherto undifferentiated words (धातु / वर्ण समास) used as both - verbs and nouns. In chronology of evolution of speech, डुकृञ् revolution occurred after [[संज्ञा revolution]] and before evolution of [[तिङ्]].
