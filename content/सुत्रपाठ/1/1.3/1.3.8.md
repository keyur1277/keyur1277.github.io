लशक्वतद्धिते
[[1.3.7]] - [[1.3.9]] 
SV:  ल-श-कु ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , अतद्धिते ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV:  उपदेशे [[1.3.2]] , इत् [[1.3.2]]  , आदिः [[1.3.5]] , प्रत्ययस्य [[1.3.6]] 
Language Meaning: The letters ल्, श्, क्, ख्, ग्, घ्, ङ् at the beginning of the औपदेशिक form of a non-तद्धित प्रत्यय are called इत्



Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
Except in "carry that" sense (अतद्धिते),  these are [[इत्]] in [[उपदेश]], at beginning of instructions 
( alternate - In the beginning of  [[उपदेश]], there were [[इत्]], and not तद्धित (that carried) instructions)
[[ल]] soften
[[श]] = erect/ elongate
[[क]] = maneuver
[[ख]] hollow/ empty
[[ग]] = move
[[घ]] - hunt / kill
[[ङ]] = periphery/outside
