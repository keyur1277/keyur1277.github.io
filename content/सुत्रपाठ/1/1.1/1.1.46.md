[[1.1.45]] - [[1.1.47]] 
आद्यन्तौ टकितौ
SV: आदि-अन्तौ टकितौ 
AV: none
Language Meaning: A टित्-आगम is attached before its substrate, and a कित्-आगम comes after its substrate.

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
[[आदि]] = untill roll inward
[[औ] of [[अन्त]] = at (here pull that )
[[टित्]]  = object(basket) like/that
[[Dictionary/पद/कित्]] = object(hand/maneuver) like/that
[[आगम]] = [[आ]] + [[गम्]] + [[घञ्]] 

Meaning explained: where "basket/curve like" is understood, it comes before object to be basketed/curved and where hand/maneuver like is understood, it comes after the object being worked on.
