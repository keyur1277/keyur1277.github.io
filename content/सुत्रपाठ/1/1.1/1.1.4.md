न धातुलोप आर्धधातुके 
[[1.1.3]] - [[1.1.5]]
SV: न धातुलोप आर्धधातुके 
AV: इकः  गुण-वृद्धी 
Language Meaning: The गुण and वृद्धि substitutions which otherwise would have presented themselves do not take place, when such an आर्धधातुक ३.४.११४ affix follows, which causes a portion of the root to be elided

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
[[आर्धधातुक]] = instructions to carry in context other than furrow/fishing 
[[धातु]] = carry that up pull 
[[वृद्धि]] =   carried 
[[गुण]]  = push 


Meaning explained: 
as वर्ण समास : Pull (न)- at/when carry that pull up(धातु)- from sand footing (लोप) - untill superior carry (आर्ध) - carry that up into hand (धातुके)  - push (गुण) and extend (वृद्धि). 
comments: आर्ध can be read as [[ऋध्]] + [[णिच्]] + [[अच्]] also. or as [[ऋध्]] + [[घञ्]] also. Both interpretations fit in. 
Comment2: न here means only pull. Though "pull" and "inhibition" circuits are neurologically linked, meaning in this reading is restricted to pull and not inhibition . 

Literal interpretation of Linguistic meaning: when carrying that up makes it (or its part)  invisible (धातुलोप) in environment other than that of furrow - push movement (गुण) or extension (वृद्धि) does not happen.
