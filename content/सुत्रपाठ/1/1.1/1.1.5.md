क्ङिति च
[[1.1.4]] - [[1.1.6]]
SV: क्ङिति च
AV: इकः गुणवृद्धी न 
Language Sense:;  In presence of a कित्, गित् or a ङित् प्रत्यय, any गुण or वृद्धि that might happen on an इक्-letter of an अङ्ग is prohibited. 


Literal Meaning
At [[कित्]], [[गित्]]  [[ङित्]]  
no [[गुण]] or [[वृद्धि]] 

Meaning explained 
When the प्रत्यय instruction contains maneuver/hold ([[क]]), move([[ग]]) or periphery/outside([[ङ]]); do not read push/extend (गुणवृद्धी) in thje instructions separately.
