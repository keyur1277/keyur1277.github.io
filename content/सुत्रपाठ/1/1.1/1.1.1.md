वृद्धिरादैच् 
[[Introduction]] - [[1.1.2]] 
SV: वृद्धिः  आत्-ऐच्
AV: none
Language Meaning: आ, ऐ and औ are called वृद्धि

Lliteral meaning: [[व]] [[ऋ]] [[द]] [[ध]] [[इ]] [[र]] [[अ]] [[ऐ]] of [[द]] [[च]] 
wave superior roll carry water desire here in give again

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation
वृद्धि = [[वृध्]] + [[क्तिन्]]  = wave superior carry + float maneuver + inward pull = 
आ - this here / up to / untill
ऐ = here inward - stay connect
औ = here up/outward = 

Meaning explained:
when wave superior carries inward (इ) it  becomes connected (ऐ)  (This receive) and when  outward/up उ  it becomes [[औ]]  (This detach)
अ becomes आ = this becomes upto
