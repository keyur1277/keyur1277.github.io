इग्यणः सम्प्रसारणम्
[[1.1.44]] - [[1.1.46]]
SV: इक् यणः सम्प्रसारणम् 
AV: none
	Language Meaning: इक् vowels which replace the semi-vowels यण् are called सम्प्रसारण


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation
सम्प्रसारण means
य becomes इ = join becomes/means inward /below
व becomes उ wave means/becomes outward / above/up
र becomes ऋ water/blood becomes/means  superior 
ल becomes ऌ sand/soft/take becomes/means inferior
