यचि भम् 

SV: यचि भम् 
AV: स्वादिषु असर्वनामस्थाने 
Language Meaning: And when an affix with an initial य् or an initial vowel, being one of the affixes, beginning with सु etc (अध्याय 4 & 5) , follows, not being सर्वनामस्थान , then what precedes, is called भ


Literal Meaning:
	At य (join/connect) or अच (स्वर विभक्ति - direction markers - अ इ उ ऋ ऌ ए ओ ऐ औ)
	भम् (steady inside)
comments
Original instructions are only steady while connecting/joining and wheile reading directional instructions. Later day interpretations clarified what needs to be steadied - This included everything in अध्याय 4 & 5 excluding सर्वनाम 
Also steading what preceeds (in instruction) is later day addition. But like all subsequent meanings and things that survived, it survived because it made sense.
