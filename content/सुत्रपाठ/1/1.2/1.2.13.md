वा गमः 
[[1.2.12]] - [[1.2.14]]
SV:  वा ( अव्ययम् ) , गमः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: कित् [[1.2.5]], झल् [[1.2.9]] लिङ्-सिचौ आत्मनेपदेषु[[1.2.11]] 
Language Meaning: Optionally after the verb गम् , the लिङ् and the सिच् beginning with झल् consonants, in the आत्मनेपद , are कित्।


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
While at school - in sandbox environment (while teaching) (लिङ् - [[लकार V2]]) and while walking inward furrow - instructions that follow गम् - are "maneuver like" except for (inside, outside, surface, pull, push, join, wave, flow, soft/sand - ञ्, म्, ङ्, ण्, न् and य्, व्, र्, ल्)

i.e. while learning to dock, ञ्, म्, ङ्, ण्, न् and य्, व्, र्, ल् do not mean  maneuver when immediately after गम्.
