Language Meaning: and हलन्तात् 


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
सन् applied to a धातु  with directional instruction followed by [[हल्]] [[प्रत्याहार]] is considered "maneuver like" (कित्). 
If इट् is understood before सन् - "below coracle" is understood before "align/gather pull/noose" - then it is not a "maneuver like".
