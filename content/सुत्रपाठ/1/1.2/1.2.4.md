सार्वधातुकमपित्
[[1.2.3]] - [[1.2.5]] 
SV:  सार्वधातुकम् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , अपित् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) 
AV:  ङित् [[1.2.1]]
Language Meaning: An अपित् सार्वधातुकप्रत्ययः behaves like a ङित् प्रत्यय.


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
[[सार्वधातुक]] - furrow related
अ [[पित्]] = not rope /limb/footing like 
[[ङित्]] - outside/periphery like


Meaning explained 
 तिङ् - शित् - instructions related to docking and launching boat  and instruction related to punting pole are deemed like outside/periphery except when they are instructions related to footing/rope/oar.
