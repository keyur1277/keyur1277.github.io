इको झल्
[[1.2.8]] - [[1.2.10]]
SV:  इकः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , झल् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: कित् [[1.2.5]], सन् [[1.2.8]], च [[1.2.8]]
Language Meaning: सन् beginning with a letter of the प्रत्यहार झल् is like कित् after the verbs ending in इक् vowels.

Literal Meaning: [[इ]] [[ओ]] ([[क]]) [[झ]] [[ल]] 
below अपादान (maneuver) - vibrate soft/sand OR
object  अपादान (maneuver) - vibrate soft/sand

Literal meaning if linguistic interpretation
Traditional: when [[सन्]] begins with a झल् ([[प्रत्याहार]]) and follows directional instructions [[इक्]], consider it maneuver like (कित्) 
If इट् is understood before सन् - "below coracle" is understood before "align/gather pull/noose" - then it is not a "maneuver like".
