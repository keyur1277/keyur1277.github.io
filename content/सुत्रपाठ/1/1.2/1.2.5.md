असंयोगाल्लिट् कित्
[[1.2.4]] - [[1.2.6]] 
SV:  असंयोगात् ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , लिट् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , कित् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: अपित् [[1.2.4]] 
Language Meaning:  For a verb root that does not end in a संयोग, the अपित् प्रत्यय of लिट्-लकार behaves like a कित् प्रत्यय


Literal meaning of language interpretation
[[लकार V2]] - in the लिट zone of foraging 
[[अ]] [[संयोग]] - when not held inside furrow opening 
अपित् = when instruction is not  rope like 
then such actions are considered instructions to maneuver.

Meaning explained: when foraging in the inside mound zone, where marine food is accrured but not visible, when instructions are not rope related and when not संयोग (gathered inside joined opening); instruction is deemed as "maneuver"
