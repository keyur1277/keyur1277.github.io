गाङ्कुटादिभ्योऽञ्णिन्ङित्
[[1.1.75]] - [[1.2.2]]
SV: गाङ्-कुट्-आदिभ्यः अ-ञ्णित्  ङित् 
AV: none
Language Meaning:  The non-ञित् and non-णित् प्रत्ययाः attached to the - (1) the 'गाङ्' आदेश (that happens to the धातु 'इङ् अध्ययने'), and (2) the verbs of the कुटादिगण (an अन्तर्गण of तुदादिगण) - behave as if they are ङित्


Literal Interpretation
consider [[ङित्]] if - 
1. Neither  ञित् nor  णित् instruction
2.  'गाङ्' आदेश to इङ् धातु ([[2.4.49]]) OR कुटादिगण

Meaning explained:
Consider instruction to be "outside"
1. Modifier Instructions are not like surface OR like push 
2. instructions are "move till periphery" to dhatu "object periphery" OR 
3. "verb(maneuver) concave" etc
