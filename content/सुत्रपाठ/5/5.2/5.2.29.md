सम्प्रोदश्च कटच्
[[5.2.28]] - [[5.2.30]]
SV: सम्-प्र-उदः  च कटच्
AV: वेः
Language meaning: The affix कटच् (कट) comes after the words १. सम् २. प्रद् ३. उद् and ४. वि
Additional Language meaning in vartika: 
The affix कटच् comes after अलाबु, तिल, उमा, and भङ्गा in denoting dust thereof.
The affix गोष्ठच् comes after the names of animals, in denoting the places. 
When spreading is denoted, the affix is पटच्, 
When a couple is denoted, the affix is गोयुगच्,
When six is denoted, the affix is षड्गवच्, 
The affix तैलच् comes when it means the oil of it. 
The affixes शाकट and शाकिन denote 'a field where it grows', after the words इक्षु
