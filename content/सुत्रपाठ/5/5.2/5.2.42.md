from [[संख्या]] = [[सम्]] [[ख्या]]  = gathered inside hollow joined till

Meaning explained: 
तयप् meanings comes from count inside (gathered inside hollow joined) at number of knots outward connected till this knot. So तयप् indicates depth up to this float.
