[[3.4.109]] - [[3.4.111]]
SV: none
AV: झेः जुस् 
Language Meaning: For an आकारान्त अङ्ग, even when the सिच् प्रत्यय is removed, the झि-प्रत्यय of a ङित्-लकार is converted to जुस्

Literal Meaning: [[अ]] [[त]] [[ह]] 
up to float exist
meaning explained - in continuation to previous sutras, furrow is to be widened till float exists. 

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation :
Even when "furrow walk" has not been explicitly stated, if last instruction is "till/ up to", "vibrate inward" instruction in context of sand-periphery, is to be read as "widen furrow"
