यासुट् परस्मैपदेषूदात्तो ङिच्च 
[[3.4.102]] - [[3.4.104]]
SV:  यासुट् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , परस्मैपदेषु ( सप्तमी-बहुवचनम् ) , उदात्तः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , ङित् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , च ( अव्ययम् ) 
AV: लिङः [[3.4.102]] 
Language Meaning: लिङ्लकारस्य परस्मैपदस्य प्रत्ययानामुदात्तः ङित् यासुट् आगमः भवति 

Literal Meaning of सूत्र : 
join upto furrow outward coracle - leg water ऐ of gather-inside - at paddle (पद) (ए षु = to peg and hence at) - up give detach float-float periphery inward float again 

Literal meaning of linguistic meaning: 
of sand inward periphery - connect un till furrow outward coracle - at launching boat - up give up to float that - periphery inward walk
Meaning explained - at  launching boat, at लिङः, before तिङ्, यासुट् is to be read.  यासुट् is up-roll-float-that and periphery inward float. 

Literal Meaning of language meaning
[[यासुट्]] = join till align outward coracle 
[[परस्मै]] [[पद]] = At oaring to sea, i.e. launching boat
[[उदात्त]] - above give till that floats/lifts 
[[ङित्]]  = outside below float
च = again 
at [[लकार V2|लिङ]]  = at teaching place
Meaning explained: 
In context of teaching; at launching the boat; when lifted above that, when outside that; insert/read instruction of "join till aligned outward coracle"
