किदाशिषि
[[3.4.103]] - [[3.4.105]] 
SV:  कित् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , आशिषि ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: लिङः यासुट्  परस्मैपदेषु  उदात्तः 
Language Meaning: आशीर्लिङ्लकारस्य प्रत्ययानाम् परस्मैपदेषु यासुट् आगमः भवति, स च उदात्तः कित् भवति

Literal Meaning: 
maneuver inward till roll object/inward/below (erect pointed)

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: connect up to furrow out coracle/low tide टीला  (यासुट्) - at launching boat - of sand inward periphery - outward till roll float that -  "hand hold that" up to erect below pointed below 

Literal interpretation of linguistic meaning
AV: In context of teaching; at launching the boat; when lifted above that, when outside that; insert/read instruction of "join till aligned outward coracle" 
Meaning of this sutra explained: 
At [[आशिष]] = till erect (deep) furrow wave maneuver inward rope 
यासुट् is [[कित्]] - "maneuver" is deemed
