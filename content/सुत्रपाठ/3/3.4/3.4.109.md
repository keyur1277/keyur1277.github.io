सिजभ्यस्तविदिभ्यश्च 
[[3.4.108]] - [[3.4.110]]
SV: सिच्-अभ्यस्त-विदिभ्यः  च 
AV: झेः  जुस् 
Language Meaning:  The झि-प्रत्यय of a ङित्-लकार that follows सिच्, अभ्यस्त or the verb root विद् is converted to जुस्. 

Literal Meaning: [[स]] [[इ]] [[ज]] [[भ]] [[य]] [[स]] [[त]] [[व]] [[इ]] [[द]] [[इ]] [[भ]] [[य]] [[श]] [[च]] 
furrow below/inward wide/open steady connect furrow float wave inward roll inward steaady connect erect walk 
Meaning explained: continuation from last sutra - while instructing at shore, झी is to be read as widen furrow because - wide furrow (gives) steady connect (for/of) furrow float (like) wave inward rolling inward steady connect (with) erect walk. i.e. for teaching docking a ship with बहु draft requires wide furrow to similate inward wave with some depth.

Literal meaning of language interpretation:
of sand inward periphery (this was there originally) - into vibrate - do widen furrow - furrow inward walk - here steady connect furrow float - wave inward roll inward steady connect exist च (च as hence)
