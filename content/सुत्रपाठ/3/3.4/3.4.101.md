तस्थस्थमिपां तांतंतामः 
[[3.4.100]] - [[3.4.102]]

SV: तस्-थस्-थ-मिपाम्  ताम्-तम्-त-अमः 
AV: ङितः
Language Meaning:  In case of ङित्-लकारs, तस्, थस्, थ and मिप् are converted respectively to ताम्, तम्, त and अम्. 

Literal Meaning:
float - (gather connect) -(gather + high ground) - inside inward rope till inside  - float un till inside float inside float up to inside



Literal meaning of language interpretation:
When inward periphery float exist (ङितः) - 
"float furrow"तस् becomes "float un till inside" ताम्
"platform furrow" थस् becomes "float inside" तम्
"platform" थ becomes "float" त
"inward inside oar un till inside" मिप्/मिपाम् becomes "here inside (tight)" अम्/अमः
