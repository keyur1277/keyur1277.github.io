द्विषश्च
[[3.4.111]] - [[3.4.113]]

SV: द्विषः च 
AV: झेः जुस्  लङः शाकटायनस्यैव 
Language Meaning: In the opinion of शाकटायन only जुस् is the substitute of झि , in the लङ् (Imperfect Tense) after the verb द्विष् 'to hate'.

Literal Meaning: [[द]] [[व]] [[इ]] [[ष]] [[श]] [[च]] 
roll wave inward -erect walk 
Meaning explained -  in continuation to previous sutra, instruction is to walk erect into wave coming in 

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: roll wave inward erect (ष is interpreted as श) exist walk 
alternate explanation: roll wave inward top tight - again
