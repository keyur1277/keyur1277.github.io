थासस्से
[[3.4.79]] - [[3.4.81]]
SV: थासः से 
AV: टितः 
Language Meaning: टित्-लकाराणाम् विषये आत्मनेपदस्य थास्-प्रत्ययस्य 'से' आदेशः भवति 

Literal Meaning:
platform up to basin align संप्रदान (basin)

Comments
When docking a single draft boat at platform+basin set up - "platform up to basin" is to be executed as "align and receive into basin"
