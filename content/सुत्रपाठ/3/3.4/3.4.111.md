लङः शाकटायनस्यैव 
[[3.4.110]] [[3.4.112]]
SV: लङः शाकटायनस्य  एव 
AV: झेः जुस्  आतः 
Language Meaning: In the opinion of शाकटायन only जुस् is the substitute of झि , in the लङ् (Imperfect Tense) of the roots which end in a long आ।

Literal Meaning: [[ल]] [[ङ]] [[ह]] - [[श]] [[अ]] [[क]] [[ट]] [[अ]] [[य]] [[न]] [[स]] [[य]] [[ए]] [[व]]
sand - periphery (high tide line) exist - erect up to hand (dig/hold)  till  टीला (ट)  connect pull furrow join - संप्रदान wave 
Meaning explained - sand periphery - erect till hand held (raise/lift) coracle connects to pull of aligned connecting wave 

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: if instruction ends in "till/ up to", in case of tide at high tide line - lift coracle till connects to pull aligns and connects into wave - vibrate
