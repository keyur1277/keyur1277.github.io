वैतोऽन्यत्र 
SV: वा एतः अन्यत्र 
AV: लेटः   ऐ 
Language meaning: In the लेट् (Vedic Subjunctive) ऐ is optionally the substitute of ए in other places 

Literal meaning of सूत्र: 
षष्टि(wave) अपादान (float) here pull+join place
continuous connection to wave, detach float at pull+connect place  
meaning: this अ place त्र where pull connects न्य - stay connected to waves detached from float 

Literal meaning of linguistic meaning:
when coracle to shore - stay put - un till wave - receive float - at pull join place
