लिट् च
[[3.4.114]] - [[3.4.116]]
AV: none
AV: आर्धधातुकम्
Language meaning: he तिङ्-प्रत्ययs of the लिट्-लकार get the term आर्धधातुक

Literal Meaning: sand inward (from) ट also

literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: sand inward from ट also is आर्धधातुक

comments:  
There are thee लकार for space between high tide line ङ and the mound created at or slightly below low tide line ट. for only one of these, hunting is relevant. 

लुङ् is when the fish is on the shore and is to be picked up. that सार्वधातुक and does not need hunting.
लिट is when shore is water inundated and is not visible. this is when hunting is required and hence is आर्धधातुक. 
लेट is not really about fishing it is about desiring. So it ideally is neither आर्धधातुक nor सार्वधातुक
