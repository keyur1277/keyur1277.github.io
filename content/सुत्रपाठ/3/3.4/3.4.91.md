	सवाभ्यां वामौ
[[3.4.90]] - [[3.4.92]]
SV: स-वाभ्याम्  व-अमौ 
AV: लोटः एतः
Language Meaning: An एकार coming after a सकार or a वकार of प्रत्यय of लोट्लकार is respectively converted to व and अम्


Literal Meaning of सूत्र 
[[स]] [[व]] [[अ]] [[भ]] [[य]] [[अ]] [[म]] [[व]] [[अ]] [[ओ]]([[म]]) 
align/gather wave untill steady connect untill inside - wave untill at fish 
सूत्र meaning in normal language:  Keep fish in water till steady water is reached. Keep fish in wave

Literal meaning of language interpretation:
when sand detached from coracle - received float tight - "gather" is read as "wave" and wave is read as "this fish"
