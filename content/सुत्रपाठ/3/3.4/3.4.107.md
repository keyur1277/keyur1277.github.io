सुट् तिथोः
[[3.4.106]] - [[3.4.108]] 
SV: none
AV: लिङः 
Language Meaning:  In case of लिङ्लकार, the तकार and थकार present in the प्रत्ययाः get the 'सुट्' आगम. 

Literal Meaning: [[स]] [[उ]] [[ट]] [[त]] [[इ]] [[औ]] of [[थ]] 
furrow outward coracle/टीला - float below at platform 

Literal meaning of language interpretation: 
of sand below periphery - furrow outward टीला - insert "float below at platform" when you find instruction "float" or "at platform"

Comment: It seems that लिङः was area where instructions were given. But not only verbally. A physical demonstration set up was done with furrow and hence instructions while teaching were slightly different.
