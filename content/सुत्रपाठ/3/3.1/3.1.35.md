3.1.35 कास्प्रत्ययादाममन्त्रे लिटि
संधि विच्छेद : कास् प्रत्ययात आम् अ-मन्त्रे लिटि 
अनु वृत्ती : none 
Language Meaning : from कास् and प्रत्यय at अ-मन्त्र at लिट, आम्

Literal Meaning 
from stone here furrow, from reed and tumbi joint, untill inside. not to inside pull place, at (when) sand below bowl/basket. 

Literal 2:
when basket/bowl is below sand, (do) upto inside from stone aligh, reed joined to tumbi, but not to inside pull floating tumbi

Literal 3:
When sand mounds falls, do upto inside, upto stone align, (with) reed joined to tumbi connector, but not to inside pull floating tumbi.
