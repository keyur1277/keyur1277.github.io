3.1.28 गुपूधूपविच्छिपणिपनिभ्य आयः
संधि विच्छेद : गुपू-धूप-विच्छि-पणि-पनिभ्यः आयः
अनु वृत्ती : none
Language meaning : आयः from  गुपू-धूप-विच्छि-पणि-पनि

Literal Meaning
Live outward rope outward carry outward rope, wave inward knot many inward,  rope push inward, rope pull inward, here-here connect

Meaning 
connect upto (here and here) - moving outward rope outward, carry outward rope, wave inward many knots, rope pushing and rope pulling
