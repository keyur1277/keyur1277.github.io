3.1.31 : आयादय आर्धधातुके वा
संधि विच्छेद : none
अनु वृत्ती : none
Language Meaning : आय आदी at आर्धधातुक वा 

Literal Meaning
here-here connect upto roll connects
here-here superiour carry, carry upto tumbi outward stone upto wave

Meaning 
before wave rolling in joins, for superior carrying, carry tumbi with stone outward before wave
