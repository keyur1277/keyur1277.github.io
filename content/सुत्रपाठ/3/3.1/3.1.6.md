3.1.6 मान्बधदान्शान्भ्यो दीर्घश्चाभ्यासस्य

अनुवृत्ति : [[सन्]]
Language Meaning: The affix सन् comes after the verbs मान 'to honour', बध् 'to bind', दान् 'to cut', and शान् 'to whet', and long ई is the substitute of इ of the reduplicate (7.4.79 and 6.1.4) syllable. 

physical meaning : 
interpretation 1: This is too long to decipher the physical meaning. Because even if each वर्ण has 4 potential meanings (2 root meanings and 2 कीभक्ति - संज्ञा and क्रिया), total potential meanings are 4.7 *10^21 with 36 वर्ण sequence.
Interpretation 2: Though mathematically there are many meanings possible, most of those meanings converge and are not unique or random. Besides, the human mind can not perceive meanings as unique random "particle" identity but perceives meanings as "wave" like fuzzy identities, and hence, it should be possible to point towards the direction of meaning.
