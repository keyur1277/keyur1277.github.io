3.1.40  कृञ् चानुप्रयुज्यते लिटि
SV : कृञ् च अनुप्रयुज्यते  लिटि
AV : आम् 
Language Meaning : आम्-प्रत्ययात् परतः कृ लिट्-लकारे अनुप्रयुज्यन्ते. After a verb which ends with आम् , the verb कृ 'to do', is annexed when लिट् (Perfect Tense) follows


Literal Meaning
कृञ्  [[कृ]] interpreted as "do" in post oldowan era. Can also mean hand hold superior water
च - again 
at अनुप्रयुज्य = [[अनु]] + [[प्र]] [[युज्]] + [[यत्]] = here pull up नल conect up open join into tumbi  
लिट = sand mount 
Do again, here pull rope connect up and jpin to mound 

Literal Meaning 2: put नल and connector from opening of tumbi and push surface (hand hold superior surface) if sand mound disintegrates
