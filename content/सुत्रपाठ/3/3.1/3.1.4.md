3.1.4 अनुदात्तौ सुप्पितौ
संधि विच्छेद : [[अन]] [[उदात्त]] (1.2) [[सुप]] [[पीत]] (1.2)
अनुवृत्ति : none 
Language Meaing: Remove upper for सुप and पीत. i.e. not upper in case of  सुप and पीत

Literal 
Meaning Level 1
here (do) pull give till at that tumbi 
(do) furrow ropes at tumbi 

Meaning Level 3
अनुदात्तौ : here(अ) pull(न) up /outward (उ) roll (द) here+here (दा) tumbi+tumbi (rope) (त्त)  here+outward (औ = अ उ)
सुप्पितौ: gather rope+rope+inward  from tumbi 
Meaning: pull tumbies outward and align rope, rope from tumbi 

words that emerged from this process
[[अन]] = here pull, this at times can mean negation and at time can mean check if joined as in [[7.1.1]]
[[सुप]] = gather rope/limb/ align rope
[[पीत]]= roped tumbi, roped object
