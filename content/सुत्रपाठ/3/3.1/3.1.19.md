3.1.19 नमोवरिवश्चित्रङः क्यच्
[[3.1.18]] - [[3.1.20]] 
संधि विच्छेद : नमस् वरिवस् चित्रङ् क्यच्
अनु वृत्ती : वा  कर्मणो करणे
Language meaning : क्यच् from नमस् वरिवस् चित्रङ्, वा,  at करण, from कर्मण, वा 

Literal Meaning
pulled from inside wave water wave long repeat place outside; stone join again

version 2:  inward tide अपादान (marine)  wave water inward wave erect walk inward 

Meaning: If it gets pulled from inside due to repeated wavering by wave waters, (causing)tumbi  water (त्र) outside (ङ) exist; then connect stone again
