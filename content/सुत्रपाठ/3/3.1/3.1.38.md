3.1.38 उषविदजागृभ्योऽन्यतरस्याम्
SV: उष-विद-जागृभ्यः अन्यतरस्याम् 
AV: लिटि आम्

Language Meaning: The affix आम् is optionally used after these - उष् 'to burn', विद् 'to know' and जागृ 'to wake', when लिट् (Perfect Tense) follows.

Literal Meaning:
AV : (if) sand mound below (s)till inside (water) 
उ up ष point व wave इ inward द roll जा widen upto  ग live(fish) ऋ superior अपादान (भ्य steady/vertical join), अ here न्य pull join त tumbi र flow स्याम्  align join upto inside

Literal 2:
the mound is to be upto inside (water) pointing up till incoming tide widens live fish into verticle/steady join, here pull join (heap) inside till it aligns with floating tumbi 

commentary
[[अन्यतरस्याम्]]  = अ here न्य pull join म् in side स्या till (it) aligns join  with तर floating tumbi
