3.1.27 कण्ड्वादिभ्यो यक्
संधि विच्छेद : कण्डु आदीि भ्यो यक् 
अनु वृत्ती : 
Language Meaning : from कण्डु आदीि, यक् 

Literaal Meaning
Stone push beward wave till roll innward verticle connect join stone

Beward stone pushes (away) with wave rolling inward from verticle join;  (hence) join/connect/fix stone
