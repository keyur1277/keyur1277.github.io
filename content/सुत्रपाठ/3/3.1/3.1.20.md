3.1.20 पुच्छभाण्डचीवराण्णिङ्
संधि विच्छेद : पुच्छ भाण्ड चीवरात णिङ्
अनु वृत्ती : वा  कर्मणो करणे
Language Meaning : at करण, from कर्मण, वा, णिङ् from पुच्छ भाण्ड चीवर

Literal Meanning:
do rope knot  many; steady untill push (fear) again inward wave water till push push inward outsideside

knot many ropes steady for fear of push.  repeat inward wave water till inward push  push (stone tumbi) outward (out of water).
