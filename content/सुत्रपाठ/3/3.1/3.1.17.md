3.1.17  शब्दवैरकलहाभ्रकण्वमेघेभ्यः करणे
संधि विच्छेद : [[शब्द]]-[[वैर]]-[[कलह]]-[[अभ्र्]]-कण्व-मेघेभ्यः करणे
अनु वृत्ती : वा क्यङ् कर्मणो
Language meaning: क्यङ् from शब्द-वैर-कलह-अभ्र-कण्व-मेघे, at करण, from कर्मण, वा 

Literal Meaning
Erect bind throw net; wave's water, stone soft tight; here stand  flow;  stone push wave; receive fish receive hunt steady join; hand hold flow from push; upto wave; join stone outward; superior stone inside from push

Keep thrown net erect in wave's water where stone turns limp into tight standing in water;  wave pushes stone; get fish get hunt from stone water push; with wave, stone connected outward; at stone superior inside push 

Meaning: keep net erect in wave's water with stone turning lipm net tight and it stands in water; when wave pushes/hits stone, get fish hunt from push of water and stone; with wave; stone connected outside (of tumbi) ; at stone superior inside (water) push
