3.1.10 उपमानादाचारे
संधि विच्छेद : उपमानात्  आचारे 
अनु वृत्ती :  कर्मणः  वा सुप  क्यच्

Language Meaning: at comparison of behaviours, of कर्म, सुप, क्यच्

Literal Meaning : 
उपमानादाचारे
outward rope fish here+here, pull her+here, roll here+here, again here+here, संप्रदान of water.
make (उ as क्रियापद)rope fish here+here, pull her+here, roll here+here, again here+here, संप्रदान of water.
Meaning: rope makes fish pull (retains) roll against the water
