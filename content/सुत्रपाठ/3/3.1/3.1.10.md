3.1.10 उपमानादाचारे
संधि विच्छेद : उपमानात्  आचारे 
अनु वृत्ती :  कर्मणः  वा सुप  क्यच्

Language Meaning: at comparison of behaviours, of कर्म, सुप, क्यच्

Literal Meaning : 
उपमानादाचारे
outward rope inside upto, pull untill, roll/tide untill, again untill, संप्रदान of water (water is received). OR
make (उ as क्रियापद)rope inside here+here, pull her+here, roll here+here, circle  here+here, संप्रदान of water.
OR
==outward leg inside up to pull up to roll up to walk up to receive water==

Meaning: 
outward leg till inside, till pull, till (tide) roll, till walk to water
