3.1.5 गुप्तिज्किद्भ्यः सन्
संधि विच्छेद : [[गुप्]] [[तिज्]] [[कित्]] [[भ्यस्|भ्य]] [[सन्]]
अनुवृत्ति : none
physical meaning: from [[गुप्]]  (pull of domestiction rope), [[तिज्]] (whettingwhetting for sharpening), [[कित्]] (whetting for making pest), concept of  [[सन्]]  प्रत्यय is derived. Hence, [[सन्]] means pull, repetitive action - desire. 
Language meaning: [[सन्]] प्रत्यय from [[गुप्]] [[तिज्]] [[कित्]]
