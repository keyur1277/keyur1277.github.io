3.1.5 गुप्तिज्किद्भ्यः सन्
संधि विच्छेद : [[गुप्]] [[तिज्]] [[कित्]] [[भ्यस्|भ्य]] [[सन्]]
अनुवृत्ति : none
physical meaning: from [[गुप्]]  (pull of domestiction rope), [[तिज्]] (whettingwhetting for sharpening), [[कित्]] (whetting for making pest), concept of  [[सन्]]  प्रत्यय is derived. Hence, [[सन्]] means pull, repetitive action - desire. 
Language meaning: [[सन्]] प्रत्यय from [[गुप्]] [[तिज्]] [[कित्]]

 commentary
 Meaning Level 1 :;
गुप्तिज्किद्भ्यः = move (ग) outward (उ) rope (प) that (त) inward opening (ज)  stone (क) inward (इ) give(द) steady(भ) join(य) exists (ह)
सन् = align/straight (स) pull (न)
Meaning Level 2 :
Move the outward rope, inward tumbi opening, stone inward tumbi, steady join 
Move outward (गु) rope tumbi inward  (प्ति) stone opening (loop) inward (ज्कि)  steady give joint tight (द्भ्यः) straight pull 

Meaning Level 3 : 
move up rope, tumbi below, open (make opening), stone (in) below tumbi, steady join tight straight/align pull 


words that arose from this process
[[गुप्]] =  भाषायाम् = stand here+here join here+here connect here+here  inside = originally did not mean language but meant instruction, stand pointed join inside
[[तिज्]] = opening inward  tumbi inside/surface
[[कित्]] = stone inward tumbi inside
[[भ्यस्|भ्य]]=stand join = verticle join
[[सन्]] = align pull

Commentary 
This activity is the first documented instance of desire/object/objective/purpose/ईप्सित (belowइ ropeप straightस तुम्बी.) 
The step to desire something besides desires arising from the Limbic System is key evolutionary milestone. This shifts the action control to the cortex. 
This stage, in my opinion, is after cortex took control of the "action centre" and before डुकृञ् hijacked it into an infinite loop. This state where cortex is in control of what one does, and yet not slave to constant urge to do something/anything, is what is remembered in आशुतोष and भोले nature of शिव.
