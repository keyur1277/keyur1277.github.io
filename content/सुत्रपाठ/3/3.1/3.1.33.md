3.1.33 स्यतासी लृलुटोः
संधि विच्छेद : none
अनु वृत्ती : none
Language Meaning : स्य-तासी at लृ-लुटोः
Intermediate translation: 
furrow connect at sand superior bowl 
furrow connect at sand superior periphery 
float till furrow inward at sand up bowl 

Literal Meaning
basin join float desire basin, subjugate sand (do) sand from bowl 

Comment
Unlike सन आदी , लृट्, लृङ् and लुट् are not वर्ण समास that evolve as प्रत्यय. These are "concepts" - abstract understanding. They do not get added to word to change its hue, instead विकरण gets added.
Question we have is whether लृलुटोः is from वर्ण समास era or this is later day description of लकार after the concept of लकार evolved. 
So Question is whether concept of लकार, at least लृलुट, emerged from activity indicated by activity described in this सूत्र  or not.
