
तत्रोपपदं सप्तमीस्थम्
SV: तत्र उपपदम्  सप्तमीस्थम्
AV: none
Language meaning: The words referenced by the सप्तमीस्थ words of the sutras in this धात्वधिकार get the term 'उपपद'.

Commentary :  It is likely that this सूत्र has been inserted later after विभक्ति evolved. It is also possible that this was there since time earlier.
