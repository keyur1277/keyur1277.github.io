Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation
कर्तुः = [[कृ]]+ [[तृच्]] + [[ङसिँ]] or [[ङस्]] = maneuver superior timber hitch outside align /gathered 
क्यङ् = hold outside 
सलोपश्च = align detach from sand rope (equal to श्च as measure of length) erect walk
