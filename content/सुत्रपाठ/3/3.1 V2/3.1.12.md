भृशादिभ्यो भुव्यच्वेर्लोपश्च हलः 
[[3.1.10]] - [[3.1.13]] 
SV: भृशादिभ्यः भुवि अच्वेः लोपः च हलः 
AV: वा क्यङ् 


Literal Meaning
superior steady/groound erect till inward roll from steady connect - steady/ground up wave connect into walk person water from sand footing erect walk -  hard sand exists 


Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation 
भृशादिभ्यः = [[भृश]] + [[आदि]] + [[भ्यस्]] = from standing deep inside upto roll inward joins
भुवि = [[भू]] + [[ङि]] = at ground up / at ground emerged 
अच्वेः = [[नञ्]] + [[च्वि]] + [[ङसि]] or [[ङस्]] from this/here into walk person 
क्यङ् = hold outside

Meaning explained : hold outside to walk at ground into stand deep till  (wave) rolling in connects
