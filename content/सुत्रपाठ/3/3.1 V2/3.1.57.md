इरितो वा 
[[3.1.56]] - [[3.1.58]] 
SV:  इरितः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , वा ( अव्ययम् ) 
AV:  लुङि [[3.1.43]] , च्लेः [[3.1.44]] , अङ् [[3.1.52]] , परस्मैपदेषु [[3.1.55]]
Language Meaning:  For the इरित्-verbs, the च्लि प्रत्यय is optionally converted to अङ्


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
च्लि - knot sand/encircle sand/ walk sand 
is optionally read as
अङ् - this periphery/ here periphery
when inward water is understood. (इरितः)
