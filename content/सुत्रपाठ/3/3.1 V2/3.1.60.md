चिण् ते पदः 
[[3.1.59]] - [[3.1.61]] 
SV:  चिण् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , ते ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , पदः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: लुङि [[3.1.43]] , च्लेः [[3.1.44]] 
Language Meaning: चिण् is the substitute of च्लि after the verb पद् 'to go' when the affix त follows

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
[[चिण्]] = walking push 
At [[त]]  = at float/log/ tumbi 
from [[पद्]] = oar, from footing give
At लुङ् = sand peripheral 
of [[च्लि]] = sand walking 

Meaning explained
At peripheral sand, at log, sand walking becomes walking push detached oar OR 
At peripheral sand, at log, sand walking becomes walking push from rope thrown
