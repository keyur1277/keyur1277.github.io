अनुदात्तौ सुप्पितौ
[[3.1.3]] [[3.1.5]] 
SV: अनुदात्तौ सुप्पितौ 
AV: none
Language Meaning:  सुप् and the affixes having an indicatory प (पित्) are अनुदात्त

अनुदात्तौ सुप्पितौ = 
here verb(noose) roll upto here this up (औ as अ अ उ)  Tumbi-tumbi- verb(gather) rope footing below this here up (औ as अ अ उ)  tumbi


Literal interpretation of linguistic meaning
do not raise when following [[सुप List]] or "rope/footing" (पित्) instructions.
