बाष्प = [[बाध्]] + [[पच्]] = bind and carry subjugated inside + beware rope knot outside pointed 


commnts
बाष्प had meaning related to bind and carry subjugated with leg knotted outward pointed  in pre-fire era. बाधृँ+डुपचँष् 
In fire era, that same act/position got used for cooking and came to mean cooking.
