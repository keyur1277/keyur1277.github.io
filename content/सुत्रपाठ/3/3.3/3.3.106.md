आतश्चोपसर्गे
SV: आतः  च उपसर्गे 
AV: कृत्  अङ्
Language Meaning: अङ् comes after those verbs which end in long आ when an उपसर्ग is in composition and when the word to be formed is feminine.

Literal meaning: [[अ]] [[त]] [[ओ]] of  ([[श]] [[च]]) [[प]] [[स]] [[ए]] of ([[र]] [[ग]])
upto float from erect walk rope gather into water move 
Meaning: from depth  equal to erectus walk upto float gather rope into water move (move here means rope to move like fish in water)


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: 
"here periphery" अङ् is tactic for superior maneuver of that/float कृत् when instruction ends in up to and you are doing "act of rope superior furrow opening" उपसर्ग
