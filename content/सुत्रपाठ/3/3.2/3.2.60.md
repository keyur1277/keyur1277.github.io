Language Meaning: कञ् also comes after the verb from दृश् At त्यद् etc. and at अनालोचन.

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
त्यदादिषु = at [[त्यद्]] आदि - that sunk at sea etc
from [[दृश्]] = give superior javelin/erect/deep below water 
at अनालोचन = [[अन]] + [[आलोचन]] = here pull/ pull surface+ up to outside from sand superior walk 
[[कञ्]] = maneuver surface 
[[क्विन्]] = maneuver wave inward pull

Meaning explained
[[त्यद्]] = from sunk at sea, from roll superior erect deep below water, not from sand knotted superior, use maneuver surface and maneuver wave inward pull 
[[तद्]] = At pulled log rolling inward, from roll superior erect deep below water, not from sand knotted superior, use maneuver surface and maneuver wave inward pull
[[यद्]] = At (join this open + here give below), from give superior श below र, pull surface from soft encircle superior, maneuver skin, maneuver wave inward pull 
[[एतद्]]  = At (inward push float coracle fear this roll below), from give superior श below र, pull surface from soft encircle superior, maneuver skin, maneuver wave inward pull
