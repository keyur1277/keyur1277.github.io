AV: कर्मणि [[3.2.1]] खश् [[3.2.28]]
Language Meaning: खश् comes after the verbs ध्मा 'to blow' and धे 'to suck' when नासिका and स्तन  are in composition with them as object

meaning explained
use hollow erect (खश्) at pointed bowl pull (ष्टन) of carried into bowl (धेट) OR
