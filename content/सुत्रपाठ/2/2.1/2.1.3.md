2.1.3 [[प्राक्]] [[कडार|कडारात्]] [[समास|समासः]]
संधि विच्छेद = प्राक् कडारात् [[आत्]] [[समास|समासः]]
अनु वृत्ती = none
Meaning = Preeceding from (कडार) compound 
Physical = (upto limb+water hand held) (hand held curve upto flow  desire upto) (gather fish desired (in) furrow) = hold limb in water (limb refers to कडार) + till float in कडार + fishing at basin = fishing at basin is (as if) holding कडार in water till float inside 
language: beore कडार समास
