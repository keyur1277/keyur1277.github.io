बह्वादिभ्यश्च

ङीष् is optionally used in the feminine after the words बहु etc.
SV: बह्व-आदिभ्यः च
AV: ङीष्  वा 
Language Meaning: ङीष् is optionally used in the feminine after the words बहु etc.

Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation :
"outside pointed" from "contained inward exist" बहि + maneuver (कु) till inward roll (of) erect walk (size)
