जातेरस्त्रीविषयादयोपधात् 
[[4.1.62]] - [[4.1.64]]
SV: जातेः अ-स्त्री-विषयात्  अ-यउपधात् 
AV: ङीष् 
Language meaning: ङीष् comes after a word denoting a जाति or species when it does not express in its original meaning a feminine and which moreover has not the letter य् for its penultimate.


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
at [[जाति]], except from [[उपधा]]  of "join"(य),   from [[विषय]]  other than [[स्त्री]], outside pointed ङीष्
