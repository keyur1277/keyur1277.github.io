कौरव्यमाण्डूकाभ्यां च 
[[4.1.18]] - [[4.1.20]] 
SV: कौरव्य-माण्डूकाभ्याम् च 
AV: ङीप्  अनुपसर्जनात्  ष्फः 
Language Meaning: ष्फ is added in the feminine after the words कौरव्य and माण्डूक

Literal Meaning: 
[[औ]] of [[क]] [[र]] ([[व]] [[य]]) [[म]] [[अ]] ( [[ण]] [[ड]] [[उ]]) [[क]] [[अ]] [[भ]] [[य]] [[अ]] [[चंद्र बिंदी]] [[च]] 


Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation :
कौरव्य = at maneuver water wave connect = maneuver at water (of) wave connect 
माण्डूकाभ्याम् = inside un till risk of being pushed out hold toill steady ground connects up to inside
च = walk
Meaning explained: 
This is in continuation to previous sutra where risk of finding a detached sand in sandy/muddy water is discussed - use outside below rope/footing - but not उंडेलना, use foot-less top/nail 
maneuver at water wave connects, inside till afraid of being pushed out, maneuver till steady ground connects in inside, walk.
