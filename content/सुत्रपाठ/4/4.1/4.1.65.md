इतो मनुष्यजातेः 
[[4.1.64]] - [[4.1.66]] 
SV: इतः  मनुष्य-जातेः
AV: ङीष्
Language meaning: ङीष् comes after a प्रातिपदिक (Nominal Stem) ending in short इ denoting classes or races of men.


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
outside below pointed/top (ङीष्) -  of below like - verb (inside pull) join that pointed outward maneuver - opening pull accomplished.
Meaning accomplished - "outside below pointed" applies to human birth. 

मनुष्य =  [[मन्]] +[[उ]] + [[यत्]] + [[षुक्]]  = inside pull + do + join that + point outward maneuver 
doer of thinking (inside pull) + connected to that outward pointed = descendent of thinker.
