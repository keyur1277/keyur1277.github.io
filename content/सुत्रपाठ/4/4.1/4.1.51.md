क्तादल्पाख्यायाम् 
[[4.1.50]] - [[4.1.52]]
SV: क्तात्  अल्प-आख्यायाम् 
AV:  ङीष्   करण-पूर्वात् 
Language : ङीष् is used after a compound प्रातिपदिक (Nominal Stem) in which a name denoting the means herewith, is the first member and the second member is a word formed by the Particle क्त when the sense is that of 'a small quantity'.

meaning explained = from maneuvering float use outside pointed object till hollow joins this (it's) connector on sand.
