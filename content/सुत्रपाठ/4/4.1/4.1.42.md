'profession or living' २. 'a bowl' ३. 'a sack or vessel for keeping grain' ४. 'a natural spot of ground' ५. 'a cooked food or rice-gruel' ६. 'stout' ७. 'black-coloured' ८. 'blue-coloured other than cloth' ९. 'a plough-share' १०. 'a libidinous female' ११. 'a fillet or braid of hair', respectively.
