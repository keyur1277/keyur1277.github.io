[[प्रथमा]]  
These are actions to be done regarding rope/leg at underside  of boat till it connects/connected to ङ. Hence adding these makes the compound word "DOER"
सुँ = If boat has draft equal to one wave, do furrow inside. 
meaning explained - ==do== furrow inside till reed platform inside 
औ = If boat hs draft equal to two wave,  here (अ) up (उ). 
Meaning explained: ==do== place up till reed platform inside
जस् = If boat has more draft,  widen furrow
Meaning explained: ==do== widen furrow till reed platform inside

[[द्वितीया]] 
These are "desired" outcome OR manipulations inside water (कर्म). 

अम्= here inside
meaning explained: place inside water ==so==  rolling wave below join and float coracle 
औट् = here up coracle 
Meaning explained: here up coracle ==so== rolling wave below join and float coracle
शस् =  erect (deep) furrow 
Meaning explained: deepen furrow ==so== rolling wave below join and float coracle 
comments: because द्वितीया started off as "desire" marker and later evolved into object market; there is epistemological confusion between object and objective. The mind feels less "uncertainty" than what is prudent regarding outcome because language confuses between purpose and object. 

[[तृतीया]] 
These are "tools" or equipment to achieve "desire". When you don't have desired incoming tide, and you yet want to get the boat afloat, you do जुगाड़. तृतीया is that जुगाड़.  
टा = for single draft boat, up to coracle
Meaning explained: coracle floats by connecting  to water ==up to coracle== 
भ्याम् = for double draft boat, steady connect up to inside
Meaning explained: coracle floats by connecting  to water , ==up to steady connect inside== 
भिस् = for boat with deeper draft, steady inward furrow
meaning explained: coracle floats by connecting  to water ==by steady in/below furrow==
