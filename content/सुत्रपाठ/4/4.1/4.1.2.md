shore side activities in connection to boat till afloat.
Because these are shore side activities, they remain same for docking and setting sail. They remain same irrespective of point of anchorage.  Only difference is whether you are making a single draft/person boat, double draft/person boat or  multi person boat. 

Singe draft
1.       Reed platform inside – make furrow 
2.       Roll in wave that connect - Here inside
3.       Superior floaters that connect below - Up to curve
4.       Knot float up water platform – into  outside
5.       Rope net knot pull – outside furrow below surface 
6.       Nails – outside furrow
7.       Align rope that pull – outside inward

Double Draft
1.       Reed platform inside – until up
2.       Roll in wave that connect – until up curve
3.       Superior floaters that connect below – ground connected upto inside
4.       Knot float up water platform – ground connected till inside
5.       Rope net knot pull –  ground connected till inside
6.       Nails – here above gathered/furrow
7.       Align rope that pull – here up furrow

Multi Draft
1.       Reed platform inside – open/wide furrow
2.       Roll in wave that connect – erect  furrow
3.       Superior floaters that connect below – steady ground below furrow
4.       Knot float up water platform – ground connect furrow=grounded in furrow 
5.       Rope net knot pull –  ground connect furrow=grounded in furrow 
6.       Nails – upto inside
7.       Align rope that pull – make furrow footing / landing
