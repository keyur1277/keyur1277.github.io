 अन्यतो ङीष्

[[4.1.39]] - [[4.1.41]]
SV: अन्यतः ङीष्
AV:  वर्णात् अनुदात्तात् 
Language Meaning: from वर्ण from अनुदात्त अ, other than उपधा त, use ङीष्

Literal Meaning : [[अ]] [[न]] [[य]] [[ओ]] of [[त]] - [[ङ]] [[इ]] [[ष]]
here/this pull join from tumbi/float - outside object nail 

Literal meaning of linguistic meaning: 
when second last instruction is not float (अन्यतः) use ङीष् (outside nailed/pointed)

अन्यतः = अन्य + तसिल् = [[अन्]] + [[यक्]] + [[तसिल्]] 
Here pull + join maneuver + tumbi gathered (at) shore
