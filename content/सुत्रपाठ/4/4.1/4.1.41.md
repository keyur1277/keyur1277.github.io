षिद्गौरादिभ्यश्च
[[4.1.40]] - [[4.1.42]]
SV: षित्-गौर-आदिभ्यः च 
AV: ङीष् 
Language Meaning: ङीष् is used in forming the feminine after the words ending with affixes which have an indicatory ष् and after the words गौर etc.

Literal Meaning
morsel at (give+live) water till roll in ground connected - also - object(outside) morsel
