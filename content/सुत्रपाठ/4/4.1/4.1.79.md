गोत्रावयवात्
[[4.1.78]] - [[4.1.80]]
SV: गोत्र-अवयवात्
Av: अण-इञोः गोत्रे  ष्यङ् 
Language Meaning: After a word denoting a non-principal गोत्र , ष्यङ् is the substitute of अण् and इञ् , the feminine, even though the syllable preceding the final be not prosodially long.

Literal Meaning
[[गोत्र]] = from(move) place = from(domestic) place = from(live) place
[[अवयव]] = [[अव]] + [[यु]] + [[अच्]] = here up verb(join) surface + here knot
