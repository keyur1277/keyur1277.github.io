[[4.1.5]] [[4.1.7]] 
SV: उगितः च 
AV: ङीप् 
Language Meaning:  प्रातिपदिकानि which end with  उक् letter get the ङीप्-प्रत्यय to indicate the feminine property. 

Literal Meaning: [[उ]] [[ग]] [[इ]] [[त]] [[श]] [[च]] 
outward [[ग]] below float erect walk (as measure o depth)
Meaning explained: you will find fish below boat to depth of person-walk 


Literal Meaning oflinguistic interpretation:
After उ , ऋ or ऌ, ङीप् is is used to denote स्त्री. 
Meaning explained : from up, superior and inferior; "outside object rope" is used to insidate "gather floaters connect upto  inside"
