गोत्राद्यून्यस्त्रियाम् 
[[4.1.93]] - [[4.1.95]]
SV: गोत्रात्  यूनि अ-स्त्रियाम्
AV: तस्य अपत्यम्
Languege Meaning: When a descendant of the description denoted by युवन् -- जीवति तु वंश्ये युवा ४.१.१६३, is intended to be expressed, a new affix is attached, only after that what already ends with an affix marking a descendant as low at least as a grandson (गोत्र): but in the feminine the word is not termed युवन् and no new affix is added.
