तद्धिताः
[[4.1.75]] - [[4.1.77]]
SV: तद्+हितः
Language Meaning: The प्रत्ययाः that are being told from now onwards up to end of the fifth chapter are called तद्धितप्रत्ययाः.

Linguistic meaning: [[तन्]] [[अदि]] [[धा]] [[क्त]]  
That pull here roll/give inward be ware carry till net meneuver that
pluck roll and carry accomplished 
[[तद्]]  [[धा]] [[क्त]]  

comment - carry here is carrying in net/ hide. Hence धा (carried) becomes हि (tight below/tight object)
