पत्युर्नो यज्ञसंयोगे 
[[4.1.32]] - [[4.1.34]] 
SV : पत्युः  नः  यज्ञ-संयोगे 
AV: अनुपसर्जनात् 
Language Meaning: The substitute न् replaces the final इ of पति before the feminine affix ङीप् when the word so formed means 'a wife' who takes part in the sacrifices of her husband.

Literal Meaning: [[प]] [[त]] [[य]] [[उ]] [[र]] [[ओ]] of [[न]] - [[य]] [[ज्ञ]] [[स]] [[चंद्र बिंदी]] [[ओ]] of [[य]] [[ए]] of [[ग]] 
rope/foot verb(float+connect) water from pull - join open+surface gather inside from connection into living/moving
Meaning explained - pull tumbi connected rope outward from water, connect (occurs)  open /wide surface - align inside join widening/opening  - pull bouy connected rope from water when reaching a surface or when opening emerges in boat

Literal Meaning of Linguistic interpretation : tumbi connected connected ( of [[पति]]) , with outside rope (ङीप्), read "pull" (न) in place of inward/below (इ), into join wide/open surface (यज्ञ) -  align inside connected opening संयोगे. 

यज्ञ = [[यज्]] + [[नङ्]]  
[[संयोग]]  = [[सम्]] + [[युज्]] + [[घञ्]]
