अपरिमाणबिस्ताचितकम्बल्येभ्यो न तद्धितलुकि 
SV: अ-परिमाण-बिस्त-आचित-कम्बल्येभ्यः  न तद्धित-लुकि 
[[4.1.21]] - [[4.1.23]] 
AV: ङीप् अनुपसर्जनात् द्विगोः 
Language Meaning: ङीप् is not used when a तद्धित affix is elided after a द्विगु समास ending in a word not denoting a mass or measure; or in १. बिस्त २. आचित or ३. कम्बल्य

Literal Meaning


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
do NOT use outside rope when - 
that pulled and carried object being picked ([[तद्धित]]) (लुकि = verb(pick)  object(maneuver))

बिस्त = [[बिस्]] + [[क्त]] = stationary fishing
आचित = [[आ]] + [[चि]] + [[क्त]] = untill knotted net is done
कम्बल्य = कम्बल + यत् = [[कम्]]  + [[बुक्]]+  [[कलच्]]  + [[यत्]]  
maneuver inside + verb(bind) maneuver + maneuver pick knot = कम्बल + join that (sum it up) = कम्बल्य (refer to [[1.1.46]] )


Meaning explained - do NOT use outside rope when contained furrow is done, while knotted net is done and while making blanket, object is pulled and carried (is) object maneuvered by picking.
