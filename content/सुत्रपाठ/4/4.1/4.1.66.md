ऊङुतः
[[4.1.65]] - [[4.1.67]] 
SV: ऊङ्   उतः
AV: अ-यउपधात्  मनुष्य-जातेः 
Language Meaning: After a प्रातिपदिक (Nominal Stem) ending in short उ , not having the letter य् as its penultimate letter and being expressive of races of men, the affix in the feminine is ऊङ्।

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
outward outside from up-like at [[स्त्री]], from opening upto that of inside pull pointed/top hold.
