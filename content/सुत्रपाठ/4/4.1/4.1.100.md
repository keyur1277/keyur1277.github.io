SV: हरित-आदिभ्यः अञः 
AV: तस्य अपत्यम् गोत्रे फक् 
Language Meaning: The affix फक् comes after the words हरित etc. when they end in the affix अञ् 


Literal Meaning: [[ह]] [[र]] [[इ]] [[त]] [[आ]] [[द]] [[इ]] [[ओ]] ([[भ]] [[य]] ) [[अ]] [[ञ]] [[ह]] 
exists water inward that till toll inward  - this surface hard. 
tight water below float up to roll inward up to ground connect - here surface hard.

use "फक्" "आयनक्" when  
[[हरित]] = [[हृ]] + [[इतच्]] = hard superior surface + below float knot 
आदिभ्यः = up to roll inward connect ground 
अञः = this surface 
meaning explained: when hard above ground surface knots below float, do sudden maneuver  (that is till join pull maneuver)
