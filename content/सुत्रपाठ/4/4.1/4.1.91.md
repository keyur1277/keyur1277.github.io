फक्फिञोरन्यतरस्याम् 
[[4.1.90]] [[4.1.92]] 
SV: फक्-फिञोः अन्यतरस्याम् 
AV: अचि  यूनि  लुक् 
Language Meaning: There is optionally लुक् elision of the युवन् affixes फक् and फिञ् when a प्राग्दीव्यतीय affix beginning with a vowel is to be added.


Literal Meaning: [[फ]] [[क]] [[फ]] [[इ]] [[ओ ]] ([[ञ]])  [[र]] [[न]] [[य]] [[त]] [[र]] [[स]] [[य]] [[अ]] [[म]] 
swell maneuver swollen below from surface water 


Literal meaning of linguistic meaning: 
[[फक्]] = sudden maneuver  / sudden stone
[[फिञ्]] = sudden below surface
[[अन्यतरस्याम्]] = here pull join / other + (pejorative) float water knot + align join up till inside 
Meaning explained: from sudden stone and sudden surface below - other float water knot align and join till inside (water)
