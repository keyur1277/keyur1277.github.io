समर्थानां प्रथमाद्वा 
[[4.1.81]] - [[4.1.83]] 
SV: समर्थानाम्  प्रथमात् वा 
AV: none
Language Meaning: The तद्धित affixes, on the alternative of their being used at all, comes after the word that is signified by the first of the words in construction in a sūtra

Literal Meaning
समर्थ = [[सम्]] + [[अर्थ]] + [[अच्]] 
gather inside this water raise this knot
gathered inside here meaning platform here again 
[[प्रथम]] Reed platform inside 
वा = till wave OR till speech
