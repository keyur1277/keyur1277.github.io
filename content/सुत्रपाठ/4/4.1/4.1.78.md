अणिञोरनार्षयोर्गुरूपोत्तमयोः ष्यङ् गोत्रे
[[4.1.77]] - [[4.1.79]]
	SV: अण-इञोः अनार्षयोः गुरु-उपोत्तमयोः  ष्यङ्  गोत्रे 
	AV:
	Language Meaning: In Patronymics used in narrower sense (counting first from the grand-child), which are formed the affixes अण् and इञ् , the feminine is formed by substituting य (ष्यङ्) for the अ and इ of those affixes and adding the long आ under -- यङश्चाप् ४.१.७४, provided that they do not denote ऋषि clans and have prosodially long syllable preceding the last syllable.


Literal Meaning:
Peg at dock form (here push below surface) join from not very pointed (or here pull superior tip); moving flowing up rope float that  join from inside 

[[ष्यङ्]]  = peg 
at [[गोत्र]] = move from place (dock++)
अण-इञोः = from  (here/this push- below surface) 
अनार्षयोः = from join (not (this/here pull) superior pointed)
गुरु-उपोत्तमयोः = from join (verb(move) verb(flow) up rope from that float inside)
