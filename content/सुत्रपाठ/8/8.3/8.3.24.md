नश्चापदान्तस्य झलि 
[[8.3.23]] - [[8.3.25]] 
SV: नः च अपदान्तस्य झलि 
AV: मः  अनुस्वारः
Language Meaning: n the context of संहिता, a मकार and a नकार which do not occur at the end of a पद are converted to अनुस्वार when followed by a झल् letter

Literal meaning og linguistic interpretation:
At carried together ( संहितायाम्), except at the end of word, a न् or म् followed by झल्  becomes  [[अनुस्वार]] 
At carried together ( संहितायाम्), except at end of oar, a pull न् or inside  म् followed by झल्  becomes  [[अनुस्वार]]
