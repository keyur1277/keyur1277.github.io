झषस्तथोर्धोऽधः
[[8.2.41]] - [[8.2.41]]
SV:  झषः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , तथोः ( षष्ठी-द्विवचनम् ) , धः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , अधः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: none
Language Meaning: तकार and थकार that follows a [[झष्]] letter is converted to धकार. But this conversion does not happen for the verb root 'धा'

literal interpretation of linguistic meaning
झत = झ  ध = vibrating float becomes vibrating carry
झथ = झध vivrating platform becomes vibrating carried
भत = भध steady float becomes steady carried
भथ = भध steady platform becomes steady carried
घत = घध hunt float becomes hunt carried
घथ = घध hunt platform becomes hunt carries 
ढत = ढध = useless/ failed float means failed carried
ढथ = ढध failed platform means failed carried
धत = धध carried float means carried carry  (overflowing carried)
धथ = धध carried platform means carried carried (overflowing carried)

* Exception: This does not apply if the preceding consonant belongs to the root धा (dhā).
