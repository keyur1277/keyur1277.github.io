SV:  ओदितः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , च ( अव्ययम् ) 
AV:  निष्ठातः [[8.2.42]]  , नः [[8.2.42]] 
Language Meaning: For the त of the निष्ठा there is substituted न after a root which has an indicatory ओ in the धातुपाठ


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
from "Detach like" ओदितः
"Pull" is substitute for [[निष्ठा]]  (pull inward pointed hole in ground - 
[[क्तवतु]] = maneuver log wave verb(float)
[[क्त]] = maneuver log)

Meaning explained
When धातु instructs to "detach", further instructions of "pull into pointed hole in ground" are converted to "pull."
Following two instructions are considered - "pull into pointed hole in ground"
1. Maneuver that / maneuver log
2. Maneuver log - person verb(that)
