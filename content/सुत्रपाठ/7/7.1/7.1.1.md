7.1.1 युवोरनाकौ
[[7.1.2]] 
पदच्छेद :  यु-वः अन-अकौ
अनुवृत्ती : अङ्गात्
Physical : [[अङ्ग|अङ्गात्]] [[यु]] [[व]] [[अन]] [[अक]]
[[अङ्ग|अङ्गात्]] = here periphery move untill that
[[यु]] = connect outward
[[अन]] (becomes) pull here
[[व]] = wave 
[[अक]] becomes here hand held (( in context of obtain by wave movement)) OR here stone (in context of wave as in wave of sea/vibrating movement against which precaution is needed)

commentary
when there an an outside movement (अङ्ग), connect outward/make connect becomes pull here AND wave becomes hold here.
