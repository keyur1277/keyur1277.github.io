युवोरनाकौ
[[6.4.175]] - [[7.1.2]]
SV: यु-वोः   अन-अकौ 
AV: none
Language Meaning:  The यु and वु belonging to a प्रत्यय are converted respectively to अन and अक.

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
verb(connect) (यु) is converted "this pull" (अन) 
verb(wave) (वु) is converted to "this maneuver" (अक)
