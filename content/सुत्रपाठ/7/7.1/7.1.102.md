उदोष्ठ्यपूर्वस्य
[[7.1.101]] - [[7.1.103]] 
SV: उत्  ओष्ठ्यपूर्वस्य  
AV: ॠतः  धातोः
Language Meaning:  If a verb root ends in a दीर्घ ॠकार before which an ओष्ठ्य letter is present, the ॠकार of that verb root is converted to उकार



Literal Meaning
ॠ (superior) becomes उ up when it follows प फ ब भ म - rope/footing, 
footing/rope superior = footing/rope up 
sudden superior =non existent but would have meant sudden up
contained superior = verb(contain) 
steady superior = verb(steady)
