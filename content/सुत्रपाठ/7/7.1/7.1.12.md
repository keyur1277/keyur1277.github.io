टाङसिङसामिनात्स्याः
[[7.1.11]] - [[7.1.13]] 
SV:  टा-ङसि-ङसाम् ( षष्ठी-बहुवचनम् ) , इन-आत्-स्याः ( प्रथमा-बहुवचनम् ) 
AV:  अतः [[7.1.9]] 
Language Meaning:  The टा, ङसिँ, and ङस् प्रत्ययाः attached to an अदन्त अङ्ग are converted respectively to इन, आत् and स्य


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
refer [[सुप List]] for context
from अत् 
[[अ]] + [[टा]] becomes [[अ]] [[इन्]] = "this/here upto curve" (अ+टा) (context is superior log that below/inward joins) becomes "here/this inward pull" (अ+इन) that in turn gets [[गुण]] - moves pushed - and becomes एन (receive pull)
[[अ]] + [[ङसि]] becomes [[अ]] + [[आत्]] = "This outside object(furrow)" (in context of footing surface enclosure/encircle) becomes "this untill float/log" 
[[अ]] + [[ङस्]] becomes [[अ]] +  [[स्य]] = "this outside gathered" (in context of nails and hooks) becomes "align join"
