ह्रस्वनद्यापो नुट् 
[[7.1.53]] - [[7.1.55]] 
SV: ह्रस्व-नदी-आपः नुट् 
AV: आमि 
Language meaning: ह्रस्वन्तात्, नदीसंज्ञकात्, आबन्तात् परस्य आम्-प्रत्ययस्य नुट्-आगमः भवति 


Lieral Meaning: [[ह]] [[र]] [[स]] [[व]] [[न]] [[द]] [[य]] [[अ]] [[ओ]] of  [[प]] [[न]] [[उ]] [[ट]]
tight water gathered wave pull roll connect till detached from footing pull up coracle untill inside
Meaning explained: when inward rolling in wave has created tight/shallow water furrow/wave; pull up coracle untill inside untill footing is detached. 

Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation:
after नदी (दीर्घ ई or ऊ at end and always feminine words) , आप  ending and ह्रस्व ending; in place of आम्, read "pull up coracle" [[नुट्]]
