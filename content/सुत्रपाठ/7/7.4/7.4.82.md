गुणो यङ्लुकोः 
[[7.4.81]] - [[7.4.83]]
SV: गुणः यङ्लुकोः
AV: अभ्यासस्य 
Language Meaning: गुण is substituted for the final of इ and उ (with their long) of a reduplicate when the Intensive character यङ् follows and also when it is elided

Literal Meaning
गुणः यङ्लुक : verb(move) push join periphery invisible (accepting लुक as a word having referred meaning in stead of वर्ण समास meaning)
[[गुण]] = verb(move) + push 
[[यङ्]] = join outside 
[[लुक]] = invisible

Meaning explained: with "join outside" instruction इ is to be read as संप्रदान and उ as अपादान. even when "join outside" instruction is not visible/explicite.
