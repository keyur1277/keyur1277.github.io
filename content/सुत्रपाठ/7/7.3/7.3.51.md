इसुसुक्तान्तात् कः 
[[7.2.50]] - [[7.2.52]] 
SV:  इस्-उस्-उक्-त-अन्तात् ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , कः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: ठस्य  [[7.3.50]] 
Language Meaning: A ठकार present at the beginning of a प्रत्यय is converted to 'क्' for an इस्-प्रत्ययान्त अङ्ग, उस्-प्रत्ययान्त अङ्ग, उक्-वर्णान्त अङ्ग and तकारान्त अङ्ग

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
 use stone as ठ when ठ follows  - 
इस् + ठ = इस्  + क = below aligned foundation  becomes  below aligned stone
उस् = above gathered foundation  becomes  above gathered stone
उ = above foundation  becomes above stone 
ऊ = above foundation  becomes above stone 
ऋ superior foundation  becomes superior stone
ऌ inferior foundation is read as inferior stone
