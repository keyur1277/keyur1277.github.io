SV:  घेः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , ङिति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: गुणः [[7.3.108]] 
Language Meaning:  A घि-संज्ञक अङ्ग gets a गुणादेश in presence of a ङित्-प्रत्यय

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of [[घि]]  - of nearby objects 
At ङित् = at "outside/periphery" instruction
[[गुण]] - move by push occurs 

Meaning explained
when "outside/periphery" instructions are applied to nearby object, move by push occurs.
