उतो वृद्धिर्लुकि हलि 
[[7.3.88]] - [[7.3.90]]
SV: उतः  वृद्धिः लुकि हलि 
AV: पिति  सार्वधातुके 
Language meaning: A root ending in short उ , which has no Present characteristic (i.e. the विकरण is dropped by लुक् elision) gets वृद्धि before a पित् सार्वधातुक affix beginning with a consonant, but not if the अङ्ग (stem) is reduplicated.

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation
when attaching a rope like (पीत) to above/up  (उ) , up (उ) is to be converted to at (औ - अ अ उ up to up)
