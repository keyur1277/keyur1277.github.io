पुगन्तलघूपधस्य च
[[7.3.85]] - [[7.3.87]] 
SV: पुगन्तलघूपधस्य  च
AV: गुणः सार्वधातुक-आर्धधातुकयोः 
Language Meaning: गुण is substituted before a सार्वधातुक and an आर्धधातुक affix for the इक् vowels of the Causative अङ्ग (stems) which take the augment प् and for the short penultimate vowel of a root which ends in a single consonant.

Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation:
convert to अ, ए, ओ (here-संप्रदान-अपादान) when "verb(rope) moving pull-that" and "sand verb(hunt) carried" walk
