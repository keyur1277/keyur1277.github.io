7.3.50 ठस्येकः
संधि विच्छेद : ठस्य इकः
अनुवृत्ती : अङ्गात्
Language meaning : of ठ, इक्, at [[अङ्ग]]
physical (लौकिक) meaning : At what goes to outside([[अङ्ग]]), instruction of foundation ([[ठ]]) becomes inward stone ([[इक्]])

commentary
[[ठ]] includes following प्रत्यय 
[[ठक्]] = unsderside stone
[[ठञ्]] = underside surface
[[ठण्]] = push underside
[[ठन्]] underside pull
