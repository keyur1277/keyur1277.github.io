सिचि वृद्धिः परस्मैपदेषु 
[[7.1.103]] [[7.2.2]]
SV: none
AV: none
Language meaning: An अङ्ग ending in the सिच्-प्रत्यय gets a वृद्धि आदेश in presence of a प्रत्यय belonging to परस्मैपद

Literal interpretation of linguistic meaning: 
At "furrow walk" (सिच), that is a substitute for "walk+sand+below" (च्लि), while "oar water of furrow inside" (परस्मै) oar into give tip outward(पदेषु);  "below" (इ)  becomes "here connect" (ऐ as अ+ए);  and "above" (उ)  becomes "here detach" (औ as अ+ओ) ; "this" (अ) becomes "upto" (आ); "Superior" (ऋ) becomes  "upto water" (आर)

	Meaning in simple English : In the channel: reach the oar to the water, thrust it down, and draw it inward — that's the power stroke. Then push the oar's tip back out and forward — that's the recovery stroke. Repeat: pull in, push out, pull in, push out — that's how you row a boat out to sea.
