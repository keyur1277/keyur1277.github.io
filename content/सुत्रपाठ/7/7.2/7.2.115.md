7.2.115 अचो ञ्णिति
संधि विच्छेद : अचः णित् ञित्
अनु वृत्ती : वृद्धि अङ्गस्य
Language Meaning: at णित् ञित्, of अचः, of [[अङ्ग]], [[वृद्धि]]

Literal Meaning
At push inward that and at surface (hyde/cloth/net); of what goes outward; of स्वर; do वृद्धि

Commentary
Why अच and not अक ? There is no वृद्धि possible for अ ओ ऐ औ in any case? 
अच also refers to repetition. Repetition causes वृद्धि to be conveyed.
