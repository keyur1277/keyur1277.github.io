7.2.115 अचो ञ्णिति
SV : अचः णित् ञित् 
AV : वृद्धि अङ्गस्य
Language Meaning: at णित् ञित्, of अचः, of [[अङ्ग]], [[वृद्धि]]

Literal Meaning
At push inward that and at surface (hide/cloth/net); of what goes peripheral (अङ्ग); of स्वर; do [[वृद्धि]] 


Commentary
Why अच and not अक ? There is no वृद्धि possible for अ ओ ऐ औ in any case? 
अच also refers to repetition - "This again/ here again". Repetition causes वृद्धि to be conveyed.
