उदितो वा
[[7.2.55]] - [[7.2.57]]
SV:  उदितः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , वा ( अव्ययम् ) 
AV:  इट् [[7.2.35]], क्त्वि [[7.2.55]] 
Language Meaning:  क्त्वा optionally gets the इट् augment after a root which has an indicatory short उ

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
if up/above is understood inधातु, [[क्त्वा]]  is to be understood with इट् (आगम), [[वा]]

Meaning explained 
If instruction "maneuver log till wave" follows instruction "above" of धातु, consider "above + below coracle + maneuver log till wave" up to wave.
