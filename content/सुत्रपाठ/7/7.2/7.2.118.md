किति च
[[7.2.117]] - [[7.2.119]] 
SV: किति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , च ( अव्ययम् ) 
AV:  वृद्धिः [[7.2.114]] , अचः [[7.2.115]] , ञ्णिति [[7.2.115]], तद्धितेषु [[7.2.117]] , आदेः [[7.2.117]] 

Language Meaning :  When followed by a कित् तद्धित-प्रत्यय, the आदि-स्वर of the अङ्ग undergoes a वृद्धि


Literal interpretation of linguistic meaning :
When instructions to maneuvers follows instruction to  carry and give that ([[तद्धित]]) follows, The first directional instruction gets [[वृद्धि]]  -
अ becomes आ = here becomes upto
इ becomes ऐ  = inward becomes connected 
उ becomes औ = outward becomes at/on 
ऋ becomes आर् = superior becomes until/upto blood/water
ऌ becomes आल्= submission becomes until/upto soft/wet/limp/taken
