अत उपधायाः 
[[7.2.115]] - [[7.2.117]] 
SV:  अतः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , उपधायाः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: वृद्धि [[7.2.114]] अचः णित् ञित्  [[7.2.115]] 
Language Meaning: उपधा-अकार of an अङ्ग undergoes a वृद्धिः when followed by a णित् or a ञित् प्रत्यय



Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
अतः = of अ like / of this log 
of [[उपधा]] =of above rope carried desired/ up to
[[वृद्धि]] = extension/expansion 
At णित् ञित् = At push like, At net like 
"Here", "extends", of "above rope carried till",  At "Push like" and "net like"


Meaning explained 
When pushed or netted, above base (footing/rope) carried becomes extended from here.  

comment 
When extended inward ([[इ]]) it is joined ([[ऐ]])  and outward ([[उ]]) is left at the place ([[औ]]).
When extended float ([[ऋ]]) goes till water ([[आर]]) and submerged ([[ऌ]]) goes till sand ([[आल]]). 
When extended superior ([[ऋ]]) goes till blood ([[आर]]) and inferior ([[ऌ]]) goes till soft/fat ([[आल]]).
