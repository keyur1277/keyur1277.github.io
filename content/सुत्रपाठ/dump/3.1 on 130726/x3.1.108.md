हनस्त च 
SV: हनः त च 
AV: कृत् कृत्याः यत् अनुपसर्गे  सुपि क्यप् भावे

Language Meaning: After the root हनु 'to kill' when in construction with a case-inflected word as its उपपद and when used without a preposition comes the affix क्यप् in denoting condition and the letter त is the substitute of its final.
