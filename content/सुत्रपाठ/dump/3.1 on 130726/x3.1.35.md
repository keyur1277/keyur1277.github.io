3.1.35 कास्प्रत्ययादाममन्त्रे लिटि
संधि विच्छेद : कास् प्रत्ययात आम् अ-मन्त्रे लिटि 
अनु वृत्ती : none 
Language Meaning : from कास् and प्रत्यय at अ-मन्त्र at लिट, आम्

Literal Meaning 
hand until, gather leg water, tumbi join, join upto tumbi, (join upto roll roll), upto inside, inside संप्रदान (pull tumbi water),  sand below bowl below

Literal 3:
with both hands at the leg in water, fill in the bowl of coconut with sand below by rolling sand inside and सम-प्रदान to sand mound below
