Erect (like માથોડાં this is used here as unit of measurement) (boundary + roll - tide) (wave's water) hold-sand hard up to unbound water (sea side) - hand push+wave संप्रदान(marine) to संप्रदान(hunt) on sea side (i.e. unbound connect exist) - hand water receive push (describes swimming)
