
अभ्युत्सादयांप्रजनयांचिकयांरमयामकः पावयांक्रियाद्विदामक्रन्निति च्छन्दसि
SV: अभ्युत्सादयाम् प्रजनयाम्   चिकयाम्   रमयाम्  अकः पावयांक्रियात्   विदामक्रन्   इति  छन्दसि 
AV: आम्  अन्यतरस्याम् 

Language Meaning: The forms अभ्युत्सादयामकः प्रजनयामकः चिकयामकः रमयामकः पावयांक्रियाद् विदामक्रन् are irregularly formed in the छन्दस् (Vedas).

Literal Meaning
AV: untill inside, here pull join floating tumbi align join inside
अभ्युत्सादयांप्रजनयांचिकयांरमयामकः = here do (verticle join), coconut align till,  roll join till inside, reed wide pull join till inside, circle inward hand-hold/stone join till inside, water inside connect untill inside holds indeed
पावयांक्रियाद्विदामक्रन्निति = rope till wave connect inside sharp stone inward connect till roll wave inside give untill inside/fish, sharp stone pull pull inward tumbi inward
च्छन्दसि = again many pull roll gather inward

Literal 3: 
if still inside, pull up heap till it aligns with floating tumbi by - 
piling untill inside holds tight - upto verticle join to coconut; upto reed length; again (coconut with) stone; water inside.)

Literal 4 :
Measure height of mound by floating coconut, reed connected to it, coconut with sharp stones and water inside. Measure by rope and heap till mound aligns with tubi.
