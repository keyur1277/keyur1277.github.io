3.1.29 ऋतेरीयङ्
संधि विच्छेद : ऋते: ईयङ्
अनु वृत्ती : none
Language meaning : from ऋति (धातु mentioned in सूत्र पाठ but not in धातु पाठ), ईयङ्

Literal Meaning
superior (i.e. floating) ए (tumbi)  water inward connect outside 

Meaning
floating tumbi connection - below connect from outward (side of tumbi)
