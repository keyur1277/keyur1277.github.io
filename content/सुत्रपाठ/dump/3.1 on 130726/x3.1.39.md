3.1.39 भीह्रीभृहुवां श्लुवच्च
संधि विच्छेद : भी ह्री भृ हु वाम् श्लु वत च 
अनु वृति : आम् अन्यतरस्याम् लिटि
Language Meaning : आम् alternatively, from भी ह्री भृ हु,  श्लु वत च 

Literal Meaning 
AV : (if) sand mound below (s)till inside (water),  here pull join (heap) inside till it aligns with floating tumbi 
भी verticle below ह्री indeed  water inward  भृ stand /steady superior हु exist up वाम् wave upto inside; श्लु  make erect sand वत wave float  च again 

Literal Meaning 3:
jf sand amound is still inside water, heap inside till it aligns with floating tumbi; 
steady below, water indeed inward, stand superior, indeed up, till wave inside erect sand up, (as if) coconut on wave, again
