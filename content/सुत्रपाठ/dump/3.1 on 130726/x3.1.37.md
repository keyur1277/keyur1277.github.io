3.1.37  दयायासश्च
SV: दयायासः च 
AV: लिटि आम्
Language meaning : After the words दय 'to give', 'to go' and 'to protect', अय 'to go' and आस 'to sit', there is the affix आम् when लिट् (Perfect Tense) follows

Literal: 
AV : (if) sand mound below (s)till inside (water) 
roll join untill join untill gather indeed long circle

Commentary
Given assumption that Erectus had limited control over मात्रा and स्वरितत्व and absence of आ , Originally these were pronounced as - द अ य अ अ य अ अ स अ श च. entire thing could have taken 3 to 4 breath cycles as per my estimation based on average length of धातु being 2/3 वर्ण. No wonder लाघव was very important, not only from memory perspective but also effort per communication point of view also.
