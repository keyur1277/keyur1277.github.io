3.1.40  कृञ् चानुप्रयुज्यते लिटि
SV : कृञ् च अनुप्रयुज्यते  लिटि
    AV : आम् 
Language Meaning : आम्-प्रत्ययात् परतः कृ लिट्-लकारे अनुप्रयुज्यन्ते. After a verb which ends with आम् , the verb कृ 'to do', is annexed when लिट् (Perfect Tense) follows


Literal Meaning:
AV: untill inside
hand superior surface (reinforce surface with hand), repeat till verb(pull) leg (of mound?) in water verb(join) ज्य widen join (make base broad for the heap), संप्रदान(coconut/float), sand mound below. 

till inside; reinforce surface with hand, repeat pull till footing in water widens and gets float (stays above water line during tide), sand mound below.
