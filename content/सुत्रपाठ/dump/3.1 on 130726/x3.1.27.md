3.1.27 कण्ड्वादिभ्यो यक्
संधि विच्छेद : कण्डु आदीि भ्यो यक् 
अनु वृत्ती : 
Language Meaning : from कण्डु आदीि, यक् 

Literal Meaning
Maneuver push beware wave till roll innward ground connect - join maneuver 

Beware stone pushes (away) with wave rolling inward from ground connection;  (hence) prehensile join maneuver (यक्)
