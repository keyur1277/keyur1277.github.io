पाघ्राध्माधेट्दृशः शः 
SV: पा-घ्रा-ध्मा-धेट्-दृशः  शः 
AV: कृत् उपसर्गे 
Language Meaning: The affix श (अ) is used after the roots पा 'to drink', घ्रा 'to smell', ध्मा 'to blow' and धेट् 'to suck' and दृश् 'to see' when there is a preposition along with these roots.

Literal Meaning: rope up to hunt water hunt inside up to संप्रदान (carry)  basket give water long exist javeline exist
