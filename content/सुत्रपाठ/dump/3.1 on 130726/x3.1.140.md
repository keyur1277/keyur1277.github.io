ज्वलितिकसन्तेभ्यो णः 
SV: ज्वलिति-कसन्तेभ्यः णः 
AV: कृत् अनुपसर्गात् विभाषा
Language Meaning: After the verbs beginning with ज्वल 'to shine' and ending with कस 'to go', when used without a preposition, the affix ण is optionally used.

Literal Meaning: (wide+wave) sand inward loat/tumbi inward hand hoold align संप्रदान (pull+tumbi) अपादान(verticle+ connect)  hard push hard
