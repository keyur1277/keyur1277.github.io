चितेः कपि 
[[6.3.126]] - [[6.3.128]] 
SV:  चितेः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , कपि ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: दीर्घः [[6.3.111]] 
Language Meaning:
 चिति is lengthened before the affix कप्


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of [[चिति]] = of pick and gather
at [[कप्]] = maneuver rope/foot 
[[दीर्घ]] = Deep 

Meaning 
Maneuver with long rope while knotting a log/float
Maneuver with long rope when you pick and collect
