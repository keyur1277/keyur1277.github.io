इच एकाचोऽम्प्रत्ययवच्च 
[[6.3.67]] - [[6.3.69]] 
SV:  इचः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , एकाचः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , अम् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , प्रत्ययवत् ( अव्ययम् ) , च ( अव्ययम् )
AV: खिति [[6.3.66]], मुम् [[6.3.67]] 
Language Meaning: A monosyllabic word ending in any vowel other than अ when followed by a word formed by a खित् affix receives the augment अम् which is added in the same way as the affix अम् of the Accusative -2nd case singular


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of single directional instruction except for here, 
consider "this inside" to be instruction when followed by an instruction set consisting of instruction "empty/hollow"

Meaning explained 
"hollow/empty" refers to "inside this." Inside of what? whatever last directional instruction referred to.
