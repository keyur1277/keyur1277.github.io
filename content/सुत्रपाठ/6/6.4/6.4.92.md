मितां ह्रस्वः 
[[6.4.91]] - [[6.4.93]] 
SV:  मिताम् ( षष्ठी-बहुवचनम् ) , ह्रस्वः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: अचि [[6.4.77]] , उपधायाः [[6.4.89]] , णौ [[6.4.90]]
Language Meaning: उपधा letter of a मित्संज्ञक अङ्ग becomes ह्रस्व when followed by the णिच् प्रत्यय.



Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
If instruction to be carried out धातु has invisible "inside" म् 
The directional instruction before the footing (second last स्वर) becomes short when pushed. 

Meaning explained
when "inside push" is instruction the last directional instructions are shallow.
