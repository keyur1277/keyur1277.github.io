ति विंशतेर्डिति

SV:  ति ( लुप्तविभक्तिकम् ) , विंशतेः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , डिति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् )
AV: none
Language Meaning:  The 'ति' of the भसंज्ञक 'विंशति' is removed


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
लोप of ति of [[विंशति]] at [[डित्]] 

Meaning explained
At implicit convex डित् instruction, at भ (boat), float below (as measurement of depth) becomes invisible in "विंशति" - i.e. विंशति is to be read merely as wave inward is deep
