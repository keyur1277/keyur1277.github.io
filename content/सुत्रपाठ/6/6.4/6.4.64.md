आतो लोप इटि च 
SV: none
AV: अचि   क्ङिति 
Language meaning: The final आ of a root is elided before an आर्धधातुक affix with the augment इट् as well as when it begins with a vowel and has an indicatory क् or ङ्।


Literal Meaning: [[अ]] [[ओ]] of [[त]] [[ओ]] of [[ल]] [[प]] [[इ]] [[ट] [[इ]] [[च]]
till detach from float detach sand from footing below coracle walk
Meaning explained: walk till sand is detached from below coracle

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation: last instruction of "up to" (आ) is like sand from leg (लोप) below object(coracle)  (इटि) as you walk (च). when first instruction is (here, inward, outward, from, to, at, joined to, superior, inferior) and the instruction has "maneuver" or "periphery/iutside"
