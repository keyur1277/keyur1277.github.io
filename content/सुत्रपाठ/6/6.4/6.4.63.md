दीङो युडचि क्ङिति 
[[6.4.62]] - [[6.4.64]] 
SV:  दीङः ( पञ्चमी-एकवचनम् ) , युट् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , अचि ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , क्ङिति ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् )
AV: none
Language Meaning: युट् is the augment of दीङ् of an आर्धधातुक affix beginning with a vowel and having an indicatory क् or ङ्

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
from [[दीङ्]], Rolling/giving outside 
युट् = verb(join/connect) bowl
at अच = at directional instruction 
At कित्  ङित् =at maneuver / outside instruction 

If "Giving outside" धातु instruction is followed by a "maneuver/outside" instruction begining with directional instruction; read verb(connect) bowl when not furrow or wave gathering.
