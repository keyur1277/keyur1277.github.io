अनिदितां हल उपधायाः क्ङिति
[[6.4.23]] - [[6.4.25]] 
SV:  अनिदिताम् ( षष्ठी-बहुवचनम् ) , हलः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , उपधायाः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , क्ङिति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् )
AV:  नलोपः [[6.4.23]]

Language Meaning:  The उपधा नकार of an अङ्ग that is अनिदित् and हलन्त is removed in presence of a कित् or a ङित्-प्रत्यय.


Literal interpretation of linguistic meaning
If - 
1. last instruction is not स्वर - directional instruction
2. when second last instruction is "pull" न  (tensile)
3. when this pull instruction is not inserted on account of deemed "pull inside" on account of instruction having an indicatory "below" (इ)
Then -
 the "pull" instruction is ignored if next instruction is "maneuver" or "outside"
