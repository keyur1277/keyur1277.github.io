टेः
[[6.4.142]] - [[6.4.144]]
SV: none
AV: अत्-लोपः  डिति 
Language Meaning:  The 'टि' [[1.1.64]] component of a भसंज्ञक अङ्ग is removed when followed by a डित् प्रत्यय.

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
लोप - of "basket below" - when followed by [[ड]] 
Meaning explained - small curve (container) and contents there of  become invisible when followed by instruction related to large curve (container).
