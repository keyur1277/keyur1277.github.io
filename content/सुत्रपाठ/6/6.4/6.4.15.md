अनुनासिकस्य क्विझलोः क्ङिति
[[6.4.14]] - [[6.4.16]] 
SV:  अनुनासिकस्य ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) , क्वि-झलोः ( सप्तमी-द्विवचनम् ) , क्ङिति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV:  दीर्घः [[6.3.111]] , उपधायाः [[6.4.7]] 
Language Meaning: of अनुनासिक, At क्विप्, at झलादि-कित्, At झलादि-ङित्, दीर्घ of उपधा

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of [[अनुनासिक]] = of inside/outside/surface/pull/push instructions 
At [[क्विप्]] = At maneuvering waving rope/limb
At कित् = maneuver that
At ङित् = outside that / periphery that
At झलादि - above three instructions related to material and not directions, but material except - ह य व र - hard, join, wave water/flow. 
second last - i.e. instruction before मकार - is to be elongated 

Meaning explained
When maneuvering wavering rope, maneuvering something मकार or instruction at outside/periphery मकार; directional instruction has to be interpreted as for longer length/duration. 

i.e. maneuvering something from inside, to outside, on surface, pulling something or pushing something, duiration/length is longer.
