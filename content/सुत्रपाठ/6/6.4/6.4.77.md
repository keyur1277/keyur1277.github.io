अचि श्नुधातुभ्रुवां य्वोरियङुवङौ
[[6.4.76]] - [[6.4.78]] 
SV:  अचि ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , श्नु-धातु-भ्रुवाम् ( षष्ठी-बहुवचनम् ) , य्वोः ( षष्ठी-द्विवचनम् ) , इयङ्-उवङौ ( प्रथमा-द्विवचनम् ) 
AV: none
status: unsolved. partly solved. 
Language Meaning:  When followed by an अजादि प्रत्यय, the ending इवर्ण and the उवर्ण are converted respectively to इयङ् / उवङ् आदेशs for a) विकरणप्रत्यय श्नु, b) Any धातु, and c) The word भ्रू 


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
when inward/below/objectify इ of धातु is followed by directional instructions अच - it is read as "below/inward join outside" 
when above/up/verbalizer of धातु, श्नु, भ्रू  is followed by directional instructions it is read / understood as "above/up wave outside" (उवङ्)
