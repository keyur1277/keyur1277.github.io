यस्येति च 
[[6.4.147]] - [[6.4.149]] 
SV: यस्य ईति च 
AV:  भस्य अङ्गस्य  तद्धिते लोपः 
Language Meaning:  The अकार and इकार of a भसंज्ञक अङ्ग is removed when followed by a तद्धितप्रत्यय or an ईकार

Literal Meaning: here and below/inward becomes invisible when steady/ground is followed by "float that carry that" or "below"
