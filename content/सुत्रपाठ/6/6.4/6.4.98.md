गमहनजनखनघसां लोपः क्ङित्यनङि
[[6.4.97]] - [[6.4.99]] 
SV:  गम-हन-जन-खन-घसाम् ( षष्ठी-बहुवचनम् ) , लोपः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , क्ङिति ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , अनङि ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV:  अचि [[6.4.77]], उपधायाः [[6.4.89]] 
Language Meaning:  The उपधा letter of the verb roots गम्, हन्, जन्, खन्, घस् is removed when followed by a कित् / ङित् प्रत्यय, except for the अङ् प्रत्यय.


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of [[गम्]]-[[हन्]]-[[जन]]-[[खन्]]-[[घस]] = of move inside, hard pull, opening pull, hollow pull and hunt furrow 
लोपः
At कित्  ङित् =at maneuver / outside instruction 
At अन अङ् - at here pull this outside instruction 
of [[उपधा]] 

Meaning explained 
"Here/This" in above धातु instructions is not given any time - inside (म in  गम), pull (न in others) and align (स in घस)occurs without any pause - when maneuver / outside instructions follow.  Except when outside instruction is this outside/ here outside .
