घ्वसोरेद्धावभ्यासलोपश्च
[[6.4.118]] - [[6.4.120]]
SV:  घु-असोः ( षष्ठी-द्विवचनम् ) , एत् ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , हौ ( सप्तमी-एकवचनम् ) , अभ्यासलोपः ( प्रथमा-एकवचनम् ) , च ( अव्ययम् )
AV: क्ङिति [[6.4.98]], सार्वधातुके [[6.4.110]]
Language Meaning: of घु अस्, ए, At हि, अभ्यासलोपः च, at क्ङित, at सार्वधातुक

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
At [[सार्वधातुक]] At carrying at furrow and waves
At maneuvering and at outside
At [[हि]] - at hard/tight below/ inward
of [[घु]] [[अस्]] = of hunt outward, here gather/ here furrow
ए - read संप्रदान in place of desire/up to 
[[अभ्यास]] [[लोप]] = noun(stand here till gather) + detach sand from foot; OR ignore instruction to stand here till gather - i.e. no need to wait

meaning explained
1. "upto + hard below" is to be read as "into + hard below" of (give upto, carry upto),  when carrying at furrow, when maneuvering outside. 
2. "gather + hard below" is to be read as "into + hard below" of "here gather",  when carrying at furrow, when maneuvering outside.
