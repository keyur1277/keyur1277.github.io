धातोः
[[6.1.161]] - [[6.1.163]] 
SV:  धातोः ( षष्ठी-एकवचनम् ) 
AV: अन्तः [[6.1.159]] , उदात्तः [[6.1.159]] 
Language Meaning: of धातु, अन्त, उदात्त

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation (अलौकिक का लौकिक अर्थ)
of [[धातु]] - beware carry till surface float up pull 
[[अन्त]] = here inside that pull 
[[उदात्त]]  = above/up give till outside give till accomplished 


Meaning explained
1. of carried that - i.e. instructions, last is उदात्त 
2. of "carried till floats", "this pulling afloat" is "raised till outside"
