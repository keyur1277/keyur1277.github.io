वृद्धिरेचि
[[6.1.87]] - [[6.1.89]] 
SV: वृद्धिः एचि 
AV: आत् 
Language Meaning: When an अवर्ण is followed by an एच् letter in the context of संहिता, both of them are replaced by a single वृद्धि letter

Literal Meaning: [[ऋ]] of [[व]]  [[इ]] of ([[द]] + [[ध]]) [[ए]] of [[र]] [[इ]] of  [[च]] 
superior wave inward(roll + carry) into water inward again 

Literal Meaning of linguistic interpretation:
At एच (ए ओ ऐ औ) - वृद्धिः (आ ऐ औ) - from अ.
[[अ]] + [[ए]] becomes [[ऐ]] = "here/this receive" becomes "here connected"
[[अ]] + [[ओ]] becomes [[औ]] = "here/this from" becomes "at"
[[अ]] + [[ऐ]]  becomes [[ऐ]] = "here+ here connected" remains "here connected"
[[अ]] + [[औ]]  becomes [[औ]]  = "here + at" remains "at"
