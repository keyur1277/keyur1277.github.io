आद्गुणः 
[[6.1.86]] - [[6.1.88]] 
SV: आत्  गुणः 
AV: अचि 
Language Meaning:  When an अवर्ण is followed by an अच् letter in the context of संहिता, both of them are replaced by a single गुण letter

Literal Meaning: [[अ]] [[द]] [[उ]]([[ग]])  [[ण]] 
roll untill outward moving push

Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation
from अ, at अच (अ इ उ ऋ ऌ ए ओ ऐ औ), गुण (अ, ए, ओ)

|             |                                                                           |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| अ + अ = अअ* | Here + here becomes “here this” or “up to”                                |
| अ + इ = ए   | “here inward” becomes “into”                                              |
| अ + उ = ओ   | “here outward” becomes “from”                                             |
| अ + ऋ = अर  | “Here + superior” becomes “this bloody”                                   |
| अ + ऌ = अल  | “Here + subjugate” becomes “here soft/ here take”                         |
| अ + ए = ए   | “Here + into” becomes “into”                                              |
| अ + ओ = ओ   | “here+ here outward” becomes “from”<br><br>Later Super ceded by 6.1.88    |
| अ + ऐ = ए   | “here + here connected” becomes “into”<br><br>Later Super ceded by 6.1.88 |
| अ + औ = ओ   | “here+ at” becomes “from”<br><br>Later Super ceded by 6.1.88              |
