इको यणचि 
[[6.1.76]] - [[6.1.78]] 
SV:  इकः यण् अचि (संहितायाम्)
AV: none
Language meaning: इक् letter which is followed by a स्वर is converted to a corresponding यण् letter in the context of संहिता.


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation:
When tightened (हित) together (सम), if followed by other directional instructions - 
[[इ]] [[य]]  = below becomes join/connect/hold 
[[उ]] [[व]] = up becomes wave/speech/person
[[ऋ]] [[र]] = superior/subjugation becomes water/flow
[[ऌ]] [[ल]] = inferior/submission/submersion becomes sand
