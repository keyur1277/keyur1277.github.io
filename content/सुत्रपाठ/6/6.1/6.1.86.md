षत्वतुकोरसिद्धः
[[6.1.85]] - [[6.1.87]]
SV: षत्व-तुकोः  असिद्धः 
AV: अचि 
Language Meaning:  The एकादेश mentioned under the एकः पूर्वपरयोः अधिकार should be considered असिद्ध while doing षत्वम् or a तुक् आगम.

Literal Meaning: [[ष]] [[त]] [[व]] [[त]] [[उ]] [[क्]] [[ओ]] [[र]] [[स]] [[इ]] [[द]] [[ध]] 


Literal meaning of linguistic itnerpretation:
pointed ([[ष]]) like ([[त्व]]) and float outward maneuver ([[तुक्]])   - does not apply to "one for earlier and after" as stated in earlier sutra.
