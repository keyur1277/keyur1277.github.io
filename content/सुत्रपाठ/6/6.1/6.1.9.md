सन्यङोः
SV:  सन्-यङोः
AV: द्वे 
Langiuage Meaning : When followed by the सन्-प्रत्यय or the यङ्-प्रत्यय, a verb root that have not yet undergone द्वित्वम् undergoes द्वित्वम्


Literal meaning of linguistic interpretation 
From "gather/align pull" (सन्)  and "join out side"(यङ) into two (ए of  द्व)
