### Abstract
This paper presents an alternative hypothesis on evolution of language.

### Background - अष्टाध्यायी 
संस्कृत is one of ancient  languages of the world. To our knowledge, It is also unique to have fully codified grammar since at least 2,000 years. By "fully codified" we mean a generative grammar capable of generating - either by rule or by a listed exception - all extant words. This codification is essentially a word generation machine. Words generated from this process come out fully formed with inflections indicating their role in the sentence. अष्टाध्यायी and its associated literature encodes Inputs, Processes, Exceptions and Meta concepts for words generated. The code specifies both the composition of a newly coined word and its meaning, both derived from Inputs and Processes defined.
The अष्टाध्यायी is one of oldest known generative mechanism for words where semantic derivation is coupled with phonetic derivation.
### Limitations
Compositionality of semantic derivation coupled with phonetic derivation does not go any further beyond lists अष्टाध्यायी encodes. The system does encode phonetic components in form of शिव सूत्र, also called माहेश्वर सूत्र for  वर्ण from which all Inputs are composed. But there is void so far as semantic derivation is concerned  except for meanings of धातु and inferred meanings of उपसर्ग. 
The tradition states that meanings of धातु as mentioned in धातु पाठ are indicatory and not lexical in nature. To add, it is traditionally accepted in Sanskrit scholarship that धातु are potentially polysemous. उपसर्ग are also polysemous and same उपसर्ग can take opposite meanings depending on context. प्रत्यय on other hand have no known meanings of their own but their semantic impact is clearly defined, and that too is polysemous. 
So, the coupling of phonetic and semantic derivation that makes संस्कृत word derivational in nature, ceases to be coupled at the Input stock that अष्टाध्यायी uses. And derivation of these Inputs from constituent वर्ण works only phonetically. Semantic meanings of वर्ण are not given in अष्टाध्यायी. 
### Challenge
This situation poses a challenge: 
1.  to decipher meanings of वर्ण. 
2. To define grammar that maps processes from वर्ण to Inputs that in turn are used by अष्टाध्यायी

Corpus that अष्टाध्यायी uses as input can be considered as equivalent of hitherto undeciphered symbols of proto-Sanskrit. Where meanings of these symbol sequences are known - उपसर्ग and धातु - can be considered as Rosetta Stone equivalent, albeit with their own limitations. Input sequences where meanings are not known but its effects are known - mainly प्रत्यय and निपात, these symbols can be treateed as equivalent to Linear A or Linear B or Sarasvati / Indus script. 
#### Comparison with other decipher challenges
The meanings are transmitted over 8000 years if we consider archaeological dating of sites (Bhirrana) found on banks of now extinct Sarasvati. Meanings of words of Rosetta Stone had relatively less time to drift where as meanings of proto-Sanskrit, had longer time to drift. One possible solution lies in polysemy. The words adopt to meanings when context changes.
Besides, possibility of drift in meanings, there is limitation of ability of brain - at least with its present advanced linguistic state. Because वर्ण is morpheme & phoneme both in proto Sanskrit; explaining it with modern languages is going to be like measuring electrons with a ruler. So ideally वर्ण meanings should be explained without using वर्ण.  That is impossible because of the fact that modern brains are practically incapable of communication *sine* language. 

On other hand, we have distinct advantages compared to deciphering of hieroglyphs and Linear B. Because Sanskrit has phonetic and semantic derivational structure, Chimeric mutations are self destructive due to drift in phonetic and semantic derivations and lineage sanctity of memetic content prevails. This makes the preserved corpus more pristine that any stone carving could have been. This derivational structure gives us two distinct advantages 1. transmission is mathematical in nature and it is possible to trace back origins/inputs 2. poly semi is built in and multiplies with length of sequence - as symbols accumulate. This generates distinct check sum like pattern of meanings that is decisively non random. Enabling decipher. 

In this light, author considers it possible to decipher meanings of  वर्ण with following caveats
1. Draft in meanings is to be factored in depending on context
2. polysemi is to be used as check sum to find match in unrelated fields. This will feed into context stated above
3. Describing meanings of वर्ण with words made of multiple वर्ण has to be carefully explained - the way measuring electron with machines consisting other electrons.

### Hypotheses
This paper presents following hypotheses: 
1. Input stock, that author refers as वर्ण समास, that the अष्टाध्यायी uses to generate words with phonetically derivable form and semantically compositionally derivable meanings, are themselves in turn phonetically and semantically derived from constituent वर्ण. 
2. Each वर्ण has its own polysemous meaning rooted in architecture of brain, either of Homo Sapiens or some other distant originator predecessor in whose realm these phono-semantic association evolved for a given वर्ण. These meanings are unknown but can be deciphered linguistically. Neuro-linguistic aspect of वर्ण meaning is beyond this paper and presented to indicate that वर्ण meanings are not arbitrary but have foundation thru non linguistic means. 
3. All वर्ण समास are phonetically derived from constituent वर्ण sequentially. Sequence is derived based on semantic primacy of experience subject to anatomical phonetic constraints.
4. All वर्ण समास have compositionally derived meaning having all constituent वर्ण meanings co-existing in working memory at the same time in a physical or perceived act in real world. It is a neural network type association and polysemy is built in by very nature of simultaneity of somatic inputs to brain. 
5. Semantically वर्ण can take any and all grammar form - any लिंग, पुरुष, वचन, काल, विभक्ति, etc. 
### Rules - Hypotheses extended
1. वर्ण is either a व्यंजन or स्वर. स्वर is anatomically अ-स्पृश्य and producible with less motor control. anatomical ability to produce स्वर has evolved before anatomical ability produce व्यंजन.
2. स्वर are special वर्ण. They have their own meaning. Besides that, they function as case-marker either for the व्यंजन they couple with or subsequent व्यंजन or both. The  स्वर behave as if they are विभक्ति to व्यंजन -  Either preceding or subsequent or both व्यंजन. विभक्ति concept has evolved from this kind of usage of स्वर. 
3. While deriving semantic compositional meaning - 
	1. ह्रस्व दीर्घ प्लुत are treated as equal and interchangeable 
	2. उदात्त स्वरित अनुदात्त are treated as equal and interchangeable 
	3. ञ म ङ ण and न are are treated as equal and interchangeable except when explicitly stated. This is done because अनुस्वार, चंद्र बिंदी, is  interchangeable to any of these. 
	4. अ - schwa is considered omnipresent by default and ignored for differential meaning derivations/deciphering efforts. 
	5. All इत described in अष्टाध्यायी have real relevant meaning and a semantic function to play
#### Methodology
When author first conceived the possibility of वर्ण having their own intrinsic meaning; log before finding अष्टाध्यायी, in a burst of intense curiosity, using dictionary and brute force. The process was unorganized and anarchic untill that effort led author to अष्टाध्यायी. On encountering this text, and having some वर्ण already deciphered, it was evident what अष्टाध्यायी text actually was, though its deciphered meaning was unknown. It was clear that - अष्टाध्यायी was not prescribing word formation, it was describing already existing memory of how words were originally formed. It was also clear, to the author, that अष्टाध्यायी documented evolution of language, though not necessarily in chronological sequence. Once author discovered अष्टाध्यायी, entore effort to decipher वर्ण meanings were focused on this corpus. 
Like all decipher processes one solution led to other and progressively next decipher became easier. With network effect each next step became easier.
